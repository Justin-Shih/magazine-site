# 鄭州污水處理邁向綠色低碳：以循環經濟重構水、能與資源 作者：雲飛．譚(Yunfei Tan) 中國的總用水量居世界之冠，但人均水資源量僅約為全球平均的35%。以中國中部城市鄭州為例：它位於黃河中下游，是中華文明與黃河文明的核心地帶；這座人口1,300萬的大都會仰賴黃河流域，人均水資源量卻僅有97.6m3，不到中國平均值的二十分之一，缺水問題相當嚴峻。 在此背景下，再生水的價值日益凸顯。再生水兼具「資源」與「能源」兩種特性：它不僅可舒緩水資源不足，也可透過能源回收協助減碳，因此成為城市永續發展的「第二水源」。對污水處理廠而言，建立「水—能鏈結與資源循環」的發展路徑，已是化解水資源與能源困境、推動綠色低碳發展的關鍵做法。 ## 前瞻的理念與設計 目前，鄭州市污水處理廠的目標，正從單純符合放流水標準，轉為資源循環、能源取得與綠色低碳發展。鄭州市所有污水與污泥的處理工作，由中原環保股份有限公司(Zhongyuan Environmental Protection, Zhongyuan EP)承擔。近年來，中原環保已明確提出將傳統污水處理廠轉型為「綠色工廠」的目標。為整合上下游產業、廠內外系統，並促成資源與能源共享，該計畫聚焦三項主要工作： - **資源循環**：將污水轉為可再利用的水資源，並回收利用污泥中的生質資源。 - **能源生產**：設法將廢棄物轉為可用能源，例如沼氣與可燃氣體。 - **減少碳排放**：盡量降低處理流程對氣候造成的影響。 為達成這些工作，鄭州新區污水處理廠(Zhengzhou New District Sewage Treatment Plant, STP)因而建立，成為中原環保實踐綠色發展理念的旗艦計畫與創新案例。 這座處理廠是河南省與淮河流域規模最大、功能最完整的污水處理廠，服務鄭州市328km2區域、389萬人口。它是中國第一座使處理水達到地表水第III類水質標準的污水處理廠，每日可處理100萬噸污水。其處理水相當潔淨，主要指標為化學需氧量(COD, chemical oxygen demand)≤20mg/L、氨氮(NH3-N, ammonia nitrogen)≤1.0mg/L、總磷(TP, total phosphorus)≤0.2mg/L，適合游泳與養魚；經進一步深度處理後，甚至可以作為飲用水水源。 該廠每天可處理含水率80%的污泥1,300噸，並每日產出100萬噸高品質再生水。作為中國最大的污泥熱裂解與氣化計畫，日處理量可達1,000噸；其引進設備能在減少污泥體積的同時，萃取可燃氣體與熱能等有用能源。廠方並採取「再生水＋潔淨能源」模式，自再生水萃取熱能，為鄰近地區提供供暖與供冷服務。 ## 發展歷程 鄭州新區污水處理廠於2014年開工，2016年正式運轉，承擔鄭州市約40%的污水處理工作。自2021年起，配合「碳達峰、碳中和」國家策略，該廠積極進行低碳轉型，建構集污染削減、減碳、資源循環與能源協同於一體的現代化污水處理平台。該廠獲頒國家優質工程獎，這是中國工程領域最高榮譽之一，並入選中國首批綠色低碳標竿污水處理廠。 ## 聚焦創新 中原環保高度重視技術創新，已建立穩健的研發體系，包括國家級博士後科研工作站、國家級水質檢測站，以及多個省、市級研發平台與創新工作室。依託這些平台，該公司發展出多項重要環保技術： - 污泥轉能源技術，也就是污泥熱裂解與氣化。 - 污水轉高品質再生水技術，也就是以活性焦為核心的超低排放污水處理。 - 廢氣處理技術。 這些創新技術已為中原環保贏得重要科技獎項，例如國家科學技術進步獎二等獎。 ### 活性焦超低排放污水處理技術 為進行深度淨化，系統使用活性焦這種特殊濾材，可去除污水殘餘的微量污染物、有毒物質及過量營養鹽，例如COD；對抗生素、荷爾蒙等新興污染物，去除效果最高可達90%。經該技術處理的鄭州出流水十分清澈，水下可視深度可達5至7m。 ### 污泥熱裂解與氣化技術：污泥轉能源 污泥在高溫、缺氧環境中處理，讓有機物分解為可燃氣體。此程序產生的熱能會被回收再利用，以驅動整個系統，達成能源自給。流程包括污泥接收、乾燥、造粒、熱裂解與氣化、熱回收及廢氣淨化。這是一種高效率、潔淨、低碳且獨立的污泥處理方案：可安全、無害化處理污泥，將其減量，並轉換為可燃氣體與熱能。 ## 水資源廠 鄭州新區污水處理廠的再生水具有兩項重要天然優勢：水量穩定可靠，水溫也適中，冬季約15°C、夏季約25°C。系統運用水源熱泵，利用水溫；它如同「熱能搬運者」，可自再生水中取出溫差，通常為5°C至10°C，產生冷水或熱水，以供建築物冷卻或供暖。 依照「先用熱、再用水」原則，再生水在萃取冷熱能後，水質仍然潔淨，可作為工業冷卻水、都市清洗用水，也可補注公園湖泊或改善河川生態環境。水資源廠的目標是：用熱而不浪費水，回用水而不造成污染。 ## 綠色能源 亞洲最大的高速鐵路車站之一鄭州東站，使用本廠再生水為車站供冷與供暖，服務面積約120,000m2。2024年，系統使用約782萬噸再生水，提供相當於184,000GJ的冷熱能；與傳統空調系統比較，可節省50.7%的用電量，每年減少約11,000噸CO2排放。 此外，該廠再生水亦為鄭州金融島3.66百萬m2建築物提供供冷與供暖。2024年，系統使用約806萬噸再生水，提供相當於166,000GJ的冷熱能；相較傳統空調系統，節電51.4%，每年約可減少39,000噸CO2排放。 目前，65km再生水管線正施工中，未來將每天輸送100萬噸再生水，為鄭州東部超過1,500萬m2建築面積供暖、超過550,000m2建築面積供冷。完成後，計畫每年將供應342.4萬GJ能源，滿足鄭州市逾40%的再生能源供暖需求，紓解冬季供熱不足，並每年減少126,000噸CO2排放。 這些作法體現中原環保的核心理念：把「污水與廢棄物」轉化為電力、熱能等實用能源。該廠致力降低外部能源使用與碳排放，將自己轉型為綠色能源廠。 污泥以外的其他廢棄物也可在廠內處理。例如，每日加入200噸廚餘進行厭氧消化，可額外產生10,000m3沼氣；若加入30%的農業固體廢棄物，例如辣椒梗，熱裂解氣化產生的可燃氣熱值與氫氣含量，分別可提高20%與30%。 處理廠還規劃建置3MW發電機組，以廠內沼氣發電。加入廚餘後，每日可產生約37,300m3沼氣供發電，每年約可發電2,409萬kWh，滿足廠內15%的日用電需求，並每年減少15,000噸CO2排放；另有可燃氣發電計畫正在規劃。 太陽能板也是處理廠永續能源組合的一環。廠內的處理池、屋頂與空地均鋪設太陽能板，目前每日可發電31,000kWh，滿足全廠10%的用電需求，並減少7,171噸CO2排放。另有一部分太陽能板正在施工，完成後預期每天可發電約54,000kWh，額外滿足15%的用電需求，並減少12,528噸CO2排放。 為儲存能源，廠內正建置10MW儲能系統，可每天在尖峰與離峰時段調節約11,700kWh電力。系統完成後，可使綠電使用更具彈性、穩定性與可靠性。 為進一步提升能源效率，處理廠採取「階梯式熱能利用」方式，以產生的沼氣與可燃氣體，讓污泥處理所需熱能自給自足。 ## 淨零碳排願景 2024年，鄭州新區污水處理廠在減碳方面取得顯著成果，平均每日碳排放約36噸。隨著更多綠色計畫逐步投入運轉，預期未來超過40%的廠內用電將來自綠色能源。作為一項具有前瞻性的永續處理案例，鄭州新區污水處理廠提供世界各地污水處理廠可直接參考的模式：污水處理廠不僅可處理污水、守護人類與環境健康，也能成為水資源廠與能源廠。 作者介紹：雲飛．譚為中國鄭州中原環保股份有限公司總經理。

Circular economy , Zhengzhou’s sewage shift to green and low-carbon development With China focusing increasingly on the value of recycled water, Yunfei Tan highlights an innovative sewage treatment model that is tackling water scarcity, reducing carbon emissions and improving the environment. hina has the largest total water consumption in the world, yet its per capita water resource availability is only 35% of the global average. Take, for example, Zhengzhou, a city in central China. Located in the middle and lower reaches of the Yellow River, it is the core area of Chinese and Yellow River civilisation. This metropolis, with a population of 13 million, relies on the Yellow River basin, but has a per capita water resource of only 97.6 m%, less than one- twentieth of China’s average, and so faces a significant water shortage problem. Against this backdrop, the value of recycled water is increasingly highlighted. With dual attributes of ‘resource’ and ‘energy’, recycled water can not only alleviate water scarcity, but also contribute to carbon reduction through energy recovery, which makes it the ‘second water source’ for urban sustainable development. Therefore, establishing a development path of ‘Water-Energy Nexus and Resource Recycling’ for sewage treatment plants has become a key approach to solving China’s water and energy dilemmas, promoting green and low- carbon development. Forward looking concept and design At present, the goal of the sewage treatment plants in Zhengzhou is shifting from mere compliance with discharge standards to resource recycling, energy acquisition, and the achievement of green and low- carbon development. The treatment of all sewage and sludge in Zhengzhou has been undertaken by Zhongyuan Environmental Protection (Zhongyuan EP). In recent years, Zhongyuan EP has set a clear goal, which is to transform traditional sewage treatment plants into ‘green’ factories. To integrate the upstream and downstream industries, inside and outside of the plant, and to enable resources and energy to be shared, the project focuses on three major tasks: © Resource recycling: converting sewage into reusable water resources and reusing biomass from the sludge; @ Energy production: finding ways to transform waste into usable energy (such as biogas and combustible gas); © Carbon emissions reduction: striving to minimise impacts on the climate from treatment processes. To accomplish these tasks, the Zhengzhou New District Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) was established, becoming a flagship project and an innovative example of Zhongyuan EP implementing concepts of green development. As the largest and most fully- functional STP in Henan Province and the Huai River Basin, it offers sewage treatment for an area of 328 km? in Zhengzhou and can handle the sewage from a population of 3.89 million. The first plant in China to achieve treated water reaching Class III surface water quality, it features a daily treatment capacity of 1 million tonnes of sewage. The treated water is so clean (main indicators: CODs20 mg/L, NH;-Ns1.0 mg/L, TPs0.2 mg/L) that it is safe for swimming and fish farming, and, after further advanced treatment, it can even serve as a source for drinking water. The STP has a treatment capacity of 1300 tonnes of sludge per day (with a moisture content of 80%) and can produce 1 million tonnes of high-quality reclaimed water per day. As the largest sludge pyrolysis and gasification project in China (with a treatment capacity of 1000 tonnes per day), it has introduced equipment that can minimise sludge volumes while extracting useful energy (such as combustible gas and thermal energy). And by adopting a ‘reclaimed water + clean energy’ Activated coke grid and treated water of the Zhengzhou New District sewage treatment plant Aerial view of the Zhengzhou New District sewage treatment plant model, it can extract thermal energy from the reclaimed water to provide heating and cooling to nearby areas. (China’s use of thermal energy in wastewater treatment is explored further in the feature on p30.) Development history The Zhengzhou New District STP commenced construction in 2014 and was officially put into operation in 2016, undertaking approximately 40% of Zhengzhou’s sewage treatment tasks. Since 2021, under the national strategies of ‘carbon peaking and carbon neutrality’, the plant has actively advanced a low-carbon transformation, building a modern sewage treatment platform that integrates pollution reduction and carbon mitigation, resource recycling, and energy synergy. As a groundbreaking project, the Zhengzhou New District STP has been awarded the National Quality Engineering Award - one of the highest accolades in China’s engineering field — and has been selected as one of the first Green and Low-Carbon Model Plants in China. > Circular economy Circular economy Focus on innovation Zhongyuan EP places great emphasis on technological innovation. To this end, it has established a robust research and development system, which includes a national-level post-doctoral workstation, a national-level water quality testing station, and numerous provincial and municipal research and development platforms and innovation studios. Relying on these platforms, it has developed several key environmental protection technologies, including: © Sludge-to-energy transformation technology (sludge pyrolysis and gasification); © Sewage-to-quality reclaimed water transformation technology (activated coke-based ultra-low emission sewage treatment); © Exhaust gas treatment technology. These innovative technologies have earned Zhongyuan EP important scientific and technological awards, such as the Second Prize of the National Award for Science and Technology Progress. Activated coke-based ultra-low emission technology for sewage treatment To deeply clean sewage, a special filtering material (activated coke) is used, which can remove residual trace pollutants, toxic substances, excessive nutrients (such as COD) from the sewage, and effectively eliminate up to 90% of new pollutants such as antibiotics and hormones. The water treated with this technology at Zhengzhou is very clear, with underwater visibility reaching 5-7 m in depth. Sludge pyrolysis and gasification technology (sludge-to-energy transformation) Sludge is processed in a high-temperature, oxygen- deficient environment to decompose the organic matter into combustible gases. The heat generated through this process is recovered and reused to drive the entire system, achieving energy self-sufficiency. The process requires several steps, including sludge receipt, drying, Sludge pyrolysis and gasification project of the Zhengzhou New District sewage treatment plant pelletising, pyrolysis and gasification, heat recovery, and exhaust gas purification. As an efficient, clean, and low-carbon independent sludge treatment solution, it safely and harmlessly treats sludge while minimising its volume and transforming it into combustible gas and thermal energy. Water resource plant The reclaimed water from the Zhengzhou New District STP has two great natural characteristics: stable and reliable water volume, and moderate water temperature (approximately 15°C in winter and approximately 25°C in summer). A water source heat pump is used to utilise the water temperature. Like a ‘heat transporter’, it can extract the temperature difference (usually 5°C to 10°C) from the reclaimed water to produce either cold or hot water for cooling or heating buildings. With the principle of ‘using heat first, then using water’, the reclaimed water remains clean after the heat is extracted for cooling and heating, and can be used as industrial cooling water or municipal cleaning water, or for replenishing park lakes or improving the ecological environment of the river. The water resource plant aims to use heat without wasting water and to reuse water without causing pollution. Green energy One of the largest high-speed railway stations in Asia, Zhengzhoudong uses reclaimed water from the STP to provide cooling and heating for the station, with a coverage area of approximately 120,000 m?. In 2024, approximately 7.82 million tonnes of reclaimed water were used to provide an equivalent of 184,000 GJ of cooling and heating energy. Compared with traditional air conditioning systems, the project saved 50.7% of electricity and reduced CO, emissions by approximately 11,000 tonnes per year. In addition, reclaimed water from the STP is used to provide cooling and heating for buildings across the 3.66 million m? of Zhengzhou Financial Island. In 2024, about 8.06 million tonnes of reclaimed water were used to provide an equivalent of 166,000 GJ of cooling and heating energy. Compared with traditional air conditioning systems, the project saved 51.4% of electricity and reduced CO, emissions by approximately 39,000 tonnes per year. Currently, 65 km of reclaimed water pipelines are under construction to transport 1 million tonnes of reclaimed water per day to provide heating for more than 15 million m? and cooling for more than 550,000 m? in the eastern area of Zhengzhou. Once completed, the project will supply 3.424 million GJ of energy annually, meeting more than 40% of renewable energy heating needs in Zhengzhou, and addressing heating shortage issues in the winter and reducing CO, emissions by 126,000 tons per year. These efforts support Zhongyuan EP’s core concept of converting ‘sewage and waste’ into useful energy (such as electricity and heat). Minimising the use of external energy and reducing carbon emissions, the Zhengzhou New District STP is striving to transform itself into a green energy plant. In addition to sludge, other waste can be treated at the STP. For example, by adding 200 tonnes of food waste for anaerobic digestion daily, the STP can produce an additional 10,000 m° of biogas; and by adding 30% of agricultural solid waste, such as chilli stalks, the calorific value of the combustible gas and the hydrogen content produced by pyrolysis gasification can be increased by 20% and 30% respectively. Further developments at the STP include the construction of a3 MW power generation unit to produce electricity from the plant’s biogas. With the addition of food waste, it can generate approximately 37,300 m3 of biogas for power generation daily, resulting in Aerial view of the cooling and heating project of Zhengzhou Financial Island approximately 24.09 million kilowatt-hours (kWh) of electricity annually, which can meet 15% of its daily electricity needs and reduce CO, emissions by 15,000 tonnes per year. In addition, a combustible gas power generation project is also being planned. Solar panels are another addition to the sustainable energy mix at the STP, with process tanks, rooftops and open spaces in the plant covered with solar panels. Currently, this generates 31,000 kWh of electricity daily, which can meet 10% of the plant’s electricity needs and reduce CO, emissions by 7171 tonnes. An additional section of solar panels is currently under construction. Once completed, this is expected to generate approximately 54,000 kWh daily, which will meet an additional 15% of the plant’s electricity needs and reduce CO, emissions by 12,528 tonnes. To enable energy to be stored, a 10 MW energy storage system is under construction. This system can regulate approximately 11,700 kWh of electricity between peak and off-peak times daily. Once completed this will make the use of green electricity more flexible, stable and reliable. To further improve energy efficiencies, a ‘cascaded heat utilisation’ approach has been adopted at the STP, using the produced biogas and combustible gas to achieve heat self-sufficiency for sludge processing. Zero carbon ambitions In 2024, the Zhengzhou New District STP had achieved remarkable success in carbon reduction, achieving average daily carbon emissions of approximately 36 tonnes. With further green projects gradually coming into operation, it is expected that more than 40% of the plant’s electricity will come from green energy in the future. As an example of forward looking sustainable treatment processes, the Zhengzhou New District STP model provides a ready-made example for STPs around the world, proving that they can not only treat sewage and safeguard human and environmental health, but also act as water resource and energy plants. ®@ The author: Yunfei Tan is General Manager at Zhongyuan Environmental Protection, Zhengzhou, China.
