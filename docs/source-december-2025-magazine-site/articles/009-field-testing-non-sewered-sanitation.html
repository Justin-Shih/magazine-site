把非下水道衛生創新帶到現場測試
Innovation put to the test

中文完整摘要說明

Sonia Grego 整理非下水道衛生系統的現場測試經驗：真實使用量、進流水質、硬度結垢、分析方法、預算與社區互動，往往會推翻實驗室假設。技術商業化必須把使用者、維運與地方條件納入工程驗證。

中文全文翻譯

全球仍有大量家庭污水未經安全處理，而傳統下水道在高密度、資源有限或既有現地系統普及的地區未必可行。新型非下水道衛生系統以多階處理、泵閥、感測器和電子控制達到病原與污染物去除，更接近小型處理廠，因此現場可靠度與維運要求也更高。 Gates Foundation 在 India、South Africa 與 Thailand 設置第三方測試平台，八年間評估超過 30 種技術。經驗顯示，設計者對人數、尿糞比與用水習慣的假設常與現場不同，過載或固體不足都會造成故障；部署前也需量測硬度等基線條件，避免電極與關鍵部件結垢。 性能驗證同時需要傳統實驗室方法、現場即時儀器與調整後的分析程序。成本估算應考慮場站、人員、物流、長期能力與多技術共用平台的規模經濟。社區溝通需從選址、安裝、變更到撤場持續進行，尤其當試驗設備改善了使用體驗時，撤除系統的社會影響也需預先管理。

The authors suggest that taking into account previously unconsidered datasets and use-case constraints could contribute to the formulation of best practices. Further developments in this area could help practitioners to establish and enhance their own quality assurance pipelines. What are the prerequisites for using machine learning in data quality assurance? The use of machine learning algorithms can represent a paradigm shift compared to the use of physics-based models. Such structures can be viewed as black-box models that build trust by shedding light on some aspects of their manifestation, evaluating them against ground truth data, and coupling them to physics-based models, for example. The model’s knowledge and flesh comes from the data — the algorithm itself is just the skeleton —which makes good data quality essential. In terms of prerequisites: firstly, ground truth data is required for the evaluation of machine learning algorithms; secondly, a model’s accuracy must be examined to infer it for new data points; and thirdly, different models should be compared when choosing the best model based on the same evaluation data. In water-related research, studies are usually based on different catchment areas and scenarios, making it very difficult to compare model accuracies. It is necessary for better ways of comparing models to be established. Conclusion Machine learning has the potential to increase the degree of automation in quality control and data imputation pipelines for water sensors and other devices. Important characteristics of the technology’s potential can be broken down to better cope with real-world and data- inherited complexities; and to leverage information currently hidden in the massive amounts of data 22 | The Source | December 2025 recorded. To date, science has not yet been able to verify this potential. Comprehensive research on comparing different algorithms — in general and beyond the field of water — has not yet led to general model choice guidelines and this is an area that should be tackled. Prerequisites for using machine learning tools can differ from those of other technologies and should be taken into account when promoting these solutions. The broad applicability of such tools ultimately depends on how extensively further research is pursued across the various branches of the water sector and beyond, how effectively user needs are met, and the availability of the necessary personnel, infrastructure, and expertise. ©
