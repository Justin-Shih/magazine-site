中文全文翻譯

**IWA與AWS簽署合作備忘錄** 國際水協會(IWA, International Water Association)已與水資源管理聯盟(AWS, Alliance for Water Stewardship)簽署合作備忘錄(MoU, Memorandum of Understanding)，旨在強化合作，以改善水管理並在全球推動水資源管理。 IWA的使命是連結並支持全球水務專業人員，推進水與污水管理的創新及永續作法。透過國際網絡、出版品、專業小組及活動，IWA提供知識交流、能力發展與領導力的平台。 AWS是一個由全球企業、非政府組織與公共部門機構組成的會員合作網絡，致力於透過AWS國際水資源管理標準(AWS International Water Stewardship Standard)，促進負責任的用水。這項標準提供一套通用架構，用以推動、認可並獎勵能使地方社群、環境與經濟受益的實務。 IWA與AWS認知到彼此使命互補，也共同承諾推進永續水管理，因此合作擴大協力並促成聯合行動。備忘錄列出下列意向： - 在全球推動並強化水資源管理實務。 - 鼓勵兩個組織網絡之間的合作與知識交流。 - 創造共同活動、會議與能力建構倡議的機會。 - 支持公用事業、城市與產業在系統及流域尺度改善水管理。 這項合作反映雙方共同相信：永續水管理需要跨尺度行動，從公用事業與城市一直到整個集水區及供應鏈。藉由結合IWA全球頂尖水務專業人員網絡與AWS的管理架構，夥伴關係目標是支持更協調、更具影響力的水挑戰回應。 **拉斯洛．索姆廖迪，1943至2025年** 前IWA會長拉斯洛．索姆廖迪(László Somlyódy)於11月4日逝世，享壽82歲。 他是匈牙利水務界的傑出人物，在協會早期的2004至2006年間擔任IWA會長。索姆廖迪於2004年在摩洛哥馬拉喀什舉行的IWA世界水大會接任會長；此一角色只是他對協會更廣泛貢獻的一部分，尤其是對IWA前身國際水質協會(IAWQ, International Association on Water Quality)的貢獻。 多年來，他曾長期擔任匈牙利國家委員會主席及治理會員代表。他籌辦多場重要IWA會議，包括在布達佩斯舉行的1994年雙年大會中擔任大會主席，也為許多其他會議的議程與組織工作作出貢獻。他在專業小組和任務小組中亦擔任領導職務，其中包括IWA/IAWQ河川水質模擬任務小組；該小組開發了第一版河川水質模式(RWQM1, River Water Quality Model No.1)。 索姆廖迪曾在布達佩斯科技經濟大學及匈牙利水資源研究中心(VITUKI)擔任重要職務。他是匈牙利科學院院士，並曾擔任該院工程部主席；同時也是歐洲環境科學基金會、歐洲科學與藝術學院、國際水科學院，以及多個匈牙利與國際高階諮詢委員會（包括歐盟機構）和多份期刊編輯委員會的成員。他在許多聯合國組織登錄，曾任匈牙利污水協會會長與匈牙利科學委員會主席。 作為水務領域的領導人物，索姆廖迪曾獲Vitalis出版獎、塞切尼教授獎助、丹尼斯．蓋博創新獎、德阿克研究獎及表彰傑出科學成就的塞切尼獎。 **黃霞教授獲膜技術獎** 中國清華大學黃霞教授榮獲IWA 2025年膜技術專業小組(MTSG, Membrane Technology Specialist Group)膜技術獎。 這項重要獎項於9月16日在南韓大邱舉行的第十一屆IWA膜技術會議晚宴中，由該小組頒發。 IWA MTSG膜技術獎頒給IWA專業小組成員，以表揚其對全球膜技術研究、教育及推廣所作出的卓越貢獻。 黃教授因在污水處理與再生利用的膜技術方面作出開創性貢獻，並在研究、標準化與人才培育方面展現改變領域的領導力，而獲頒此獎。

IWA News IWA and AWS sign MoU IWA has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Alliance for Water Stewardship (AWS) aimed at strengthening collaboration to improve water management and promote water stewardship worldwide. IWA’s mission is to connect and support water professionals globally, advancing innovative and sustainable approaches to water and wastewater management. Through its international network, publications, specialist groups and events, WA provides a platform for knowledge exchange, capacity development and leadership. AWS is amembership collaboration of global businesses, NGOs and public sector institutions dedicated to advancing responsible water use through the AWS International Water Stewardship Standard. The Standard offers a universal framework that drives, recognises and rewards practices that benefit local communities, the environment and economies. Recognising their complementary missions and commitment to advancing sustainable water management, |WA and AWS are partnering to expand cooperation and enable joint action. The MoU outlines intentions to: @ Promote and strengthen water stewardship practices globally @ Encourage collaboration and knowledge exchange across both organisations’ networks © Create opportunities for joint activities, events, and capacity-building initiatives © Support utilities, cities and industries to improve water management at system and watershed levels. This collaboration reflects a shared belief that sustainable water management requires action across scales, from utilities and cities to entire catchments and supply chains. By combining IWA’s global network of leading water professionals with AWS’s framework for stewardship, the partnership aims to support more coordinated, impactful responses to water challenges. Laszlo Somlyddy, 1943-2025 Former IWA President Laszl6 Somlyédy died on 4 November aged 82. A prominent figure in the water sector in Hungary, he served as IWA President in the early years of the Association, from 2004-2006. Assuming the presidency at IWA’s 2004 World Water Congress held in Marrakech, Morocco, his contribution through this role was part of a much greater contribution to the Association, especially to its predecessor organisation, the International Association on Water Quality (IAWQ). Laszld’s contributions over the years included long service as chair and Governing Member representative of the Hungarian National Committee. He organised a number of important IWA conferences (including serving as Conference President for the 1994 biennial Congress in Budapest), and contributed to the programming and organising of numerous others. He also held leading roles in Specialist Groups and Task Groups, including the |WA/IAWQ Task Group on River Water Quality Modelling, which developed the River Water Quality Model Nol (RWQM1). Laszl6 held key roles at the Budapest University of Technology and Economics, and at Hungary’s Water Resources Research Centre (VITUKI). He was a member of the Hungarian Academy of Sciences (chairman of its Engineering Department), the European Environmental Science Foundation, the European Academy of Sciences and Arts, the International Water Academy, a number of Hungarian and international high-level advisory boards (including EU bodies), and a number of journal editorial boards. Registered at many UN organisations, he was President of the Hungarian Wastewater Association, and chair of the Science Council in Hungary. As aleading figure in the water sector, Laszl6 received the Vitalis publication award, the Széchenyi professor fellowship, the Dennis Gabor innovation award, the Dedk research award, and the Széchenyi award for outstanding performance in science. Professor Xia Huang receives membrane technology award Professor Xia Huang of Tsinghua University, China, has won IWA’s 2025 Membrane Technology Specialist Group (MTSG) Membrane Technology Award. The prestigious prize was presented by the group during the Gala Dinner of the TIth IWA Membrane Technology Conference held in Daegu, South Korea, on 16 September. The IWA MTSG Membrane Technology Award is given to a member of the IWA Specialist Group in recognition of his or her exceptional contribution to research, education and dissemination of membrane technology worldwide. The award was presented to Professor Huang in recognition of her pioneering contributions in membrane technologies for wastewater treatment and reclamation, as well as her transformative leadership in research, standardisation and mentoring that have shaped the field worldwide.
