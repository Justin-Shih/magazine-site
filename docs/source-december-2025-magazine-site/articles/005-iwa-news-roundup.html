IWA News IWA and AWS sign MoU IWA has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Alliance for Water Stewardship (AWS) aimed at strengthening collaboration to improve water management and promote water stewardship worldwide. IWA’s mission is to connect and support water professionals globally, advancing innovative and sustainable approaches to water and wastewater management. Through its international network, publications, specialist groups and events, WA provides a platform for knowledge exchange, capacity development and leadership. AWS is amembership collaboration of global businesses, NGOs and public sector institutions dedicated to advancing responsible water use through the AWS International Water Stewardship Standard. The Standard offers a universal framework that drives, recognises and rewards practices that benefit local communities, the environment and economies. Recognising their complementary missions and commitment to advancing sustainable water management, |WA and AWS are partnering to expand cooperation and enable joint action. The MoU outlines intentions to: @ Promote and strengthen water stewardship practices globally @ Encourage collaboration and knowledge exchange across both organisations’ networks © Create opportunities for joint activities, events, and capacity- building initiatives © Support utilities, cities and industries to improve water management at system and watershed levels. This collaboration reflects a shared belief that sustainable water management requires action across scales, from utilities and cities to entire catchments and supply chains. Laszl6 held key roles at the Budapest University of Technology and Economics, and at Hungary’s Water Resources Research Centre (VITUKI). He was a member of the Hungarian Academy of Sciences (chairman of its Engineering Department), the European Environmental Science Foundation, the European Academy of Sciences and Arts, the International Water Academy, a number of Hungarian and international high-level advisory boards (including EU bodies), and a number of journal editorial boards. Registered at many UN organisations, he was President of the Hungarian Wastewater Association, and chair of the Science Council in Hungary. As aleading figure in the water sector, Laszl6 received the Vitalis publication award, the Széchenyi professor fellowship, the Dennis Gabor innovation award, the Dedk research award, and the Széchenyi award for outstanding performance in science. Professor Xia Huang receives membrane technology award Professor Xia Huang of Tsinghua University, China, has won IWA’s 2025 Membrane Technology Specialist Group (MTSG) Membrane Technology Award. The prestigious prize was presented by the group during the Gala Dinner of the TIth IWA Membrane Technology Conference held in Daegu, South Korea, on 16 September. The IWA MTSG Membrane Technology Award is given to a member of the IWA Specialist Group in recognition of his or her exceptional contribution to research, education and dissemination of membrane technology worldwide. The award was presented to Professor Huang in recognition of her pioneering contributions in membrane technologies for wastewater treatment and reclamation, as well as her transformative leadership in research, standardisation and mentoring that have shaped the field worldwide.
