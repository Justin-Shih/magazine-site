Sustainability Salt River Project improves renewable energy efficiency Emerson, an industrial technology leader delivering advanced automation solutions, has completed a multi-year modernisation project at four Salt River hydroelectric dams along the Salt River in Arizona, USA. Salt River Project's (SRP’s) Roosevelt, Horse Mesa, Mormon Flat and Stewart Mountain dams generate 265 MW of hydropower - enough to power more than 60,000 homes annually. SRP modernised its legacy Excitation Systems, necessary for power generation, and integrated them with the distributed control systems already in place at its four hydroelectric dams. By standardising the system using Emerson's Ovation Automation Platform, and implementing modern control strategies, Emerson says it has reduced SRP’s operations and maintenance costs by 30%, and has cut troubleshooting time by half, helping to ensure operational continuity and strengthening grid performance. SRP’s new, fully integrated automation platform simplifies operations and maintenance by using the same spare parts, streamlining training, maintaining operator familiarity, and managing a single point of contact for automation support and service. Tampa Bay pilots water quality prediction system SkyTL has announced a joint pilot programme with Acciona and Tampa Bay Water, USA, to deploy asystem that predicts water quality events before they reach utility intake points. The initiative, which won the Acciona North America Innovation Award, integrates satellite imagery, fixed-cameras, on-demand unmanned aircraft systems (UAS), and machine learning models to provide an earlier, more accurate warning of turbidity, red tide, and salinity spikes, among other parameters, that affect the continuity of operations. Unlike traditional monitoring systems that detect changes only at intake locations, this pilot programme will analyse real-time data from the bay and open waters to predict and report potential events at least two hours before they are measured by the facility’s instruments.
