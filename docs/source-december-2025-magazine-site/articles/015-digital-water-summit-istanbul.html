中文全文翻譯

**在伊斯坦堡描繪下一波轉型** Samuela Guida描繪IWA數位水峰會(Digital Water Summit)的發展軌跡。 IWA數位水峰會已穩固地成為專注水務部門數位轉型的首要全球企業對企業(B2B, business-to-business)活動。短短幾年內，它已發展為業界領導者所稱的「全球水務社群基石」，成為技術供應商、公用事業高階主管及創新者不可或缺的會面點。峰會提供無可比擬的平台，探索數位化如何重塑水管理，以回應世界最急迫的挑戰。 活動成功反映在社群的回饋上。2024年會後的全面調查顯示，93%的與會者會高度推薦同事參加，且多數人將整體體驗評為最高滿意等級。這份亮眼成績，建立在峰會前三年有策略且審慎的發展歷程之上。 **畢爾包的基礎** 峰會最初在西班牙畢爾包(Bilbao)連續三年舉行，是刻意安排的策略階段；它提供穩定性，以培養一致敘事並為全球數位水運動累積關鍵動能。這對建立清晰、漸進的敘事不可或缺，引導水務領域從早期探索數位概念，走向轉型解決方案的實際且廣泛應用。這段期間核心主題的演變，清楚呈現產業數位旅程的成熟。 **奠定基礎（2022）：界定核心技術** 2022年首屆峰會聚焦於界定數位樣貌，並探索可能重塑本領域的基礎技術。議程深入討論物聯網(IoT, Internet of Things)、遙測、地理資訊系統(GIS, geographic information systems)及進階資料分析等核心概念。該屆藉由介紹人工智慧(AI, artificial intelligence)等關鍵創新，以及以數位孿生(digital twins)模擬水系統這一有力的初始概念，為後續討論奠定基礎。 **帶來可見影響（2023）：從理論到應用** 第二屆峰會將討論轉向真實世界影響，以及數位工具在實現永續性上的角色。議程呈現實務案例，包括利用AI進行污水管閉路電視(CCTV, closed-circuit television)檢查、使用地球觀測(Earth Observation)技術進行監測，以及探討數位轉型改善水務部門永續性的潛力。 **擴大全球影響（2024）：處理系統層級挑戰** 最近一屆峰會擴大範圍，處理系統層級挑戰與全球公平議題。討論聚焦於透過數位工具實現淨零排放、發展有利於中低收入國家的實用策略，以及利用整合資料推動整個水務部門的系統性變革。這象徵數位水邁向整體性、具有全球視野作法的重要一步。 **新地平線：伊斯坦堡，2026年的理想主辦城市** 畢爾包累積的動能，已為峰會下一個演進階段搭好舞台，並將在新的國際樞紐展開。 2026年移至伊斯坦堡，代表峰會全球觸及與影響力的策略性擴展。把這項關鍵對話帶到科技、創新與文化交會的活力新節點，是刻意希望接觸新區域、培養更具多元性的全球水務未來對話。峰會新篇章將於2026年4月7日至9日，在土耳其伊斯坦堡的哈利奇會議中心(Halic Congress Center)展開。 這項關鍵活動由IWA與伊斯坦堡理工大學(Istanbul Technical University)共同主辦。數位水峰會在伊斯坦堡舉行，將開啟新的觀點、挑戰及國際合作擴展機會，確保議程持續具備卓越品質與前瞻性。 **2026數位水峰會可以期待什麼** 隨著數位水領域由基礎探索轉向規模化實施，2026年峰會議程經策略設計以回應這個新前線。它不只延續畢爾包的對話，也在全球水務社群直接回饋的引導下提升層次，目標是為所有參與者提供最大價值、洞見與彼此連結的機會。 **經驗證的卓越形式** 峰會將保留廣受與會者肯定的成功形式。議程包括高影響力技術場次、InnoHub解決方案展示、互動式座談及為高價值人脈交流設計的社交活動。 **具回應力與前瞻性的議程** 2026年主題焦點經過規劃，以處理數位水空間最急迫的議題。核心主題包括水系統中的IoT、預測分析與AI、智慧水表、漏水偵測、管網數位孿生及資安。這份議程反映全球水務社群在2024年回饋調查所提出的需求：更深入探討AI應用、新技術投資報酬率分析，以及由先導試驗擴展至全面實施的實務案例。 **以商業為核心的生態系** 峰會的核心目的，是將技術供應商及創新者與最需要解決方案的公用事業及終端使用者連結，加速數位解決方案採用。它促成從先導專案走向擴大、全系統實施的關鍵旅程，因此成為推動本領域實際進步的重要平台。IWA數位水峰會2026提供無可比擬的機會，讓人與形塑水管理未來的領導者、理念及解決方案互動。 **更多資訊** 請將IWA數位水峰會排入行事曆，參與這項重要對話。 IWA數位水峰會，2026年4月7日至9日，土耳其伊斯坦堡。 完整技術議程將於未來數月公布。希望與這項指標性活動合作的組織，可至digitalwatersummit.org的IWA網站下載正式贊助商及參展商手冊。 **作者** Samuela Guida為IWA策略方案與參與經理。

WaA’s Digital Water Summit has firmly established itself as the premier global B2B event dedicated to the digital transformation of the water sector. In just a few years, it has matured into what industry leaders describe as a “cornerstone for the global water community”, serving as an essential meeting point for technology providers, utility executives and innovators. The Summit offers an unparalleled platform for exploring how digitalisation can reshape water management to address the world’s most pressing challenges. The event’s success is reflected in feedback from its community. A comprehensive survey after the 2024 edition revealed that 93% of attendees would highly recommend the Summit to colleagues, with a majority rating their overall experience in the highest tiers of satisfaction. This outstanding track record of success was built upon a strategic and deliberate journey undertaken during the Summit’s first three years. The Bilbao legacy The Summit’s initial three-year residency in Bilbao, Spain, marked a deliberate strategic phase, providing the stability to cultivate a coherent narrative and build critical momentum for the global digital water movement. It was essential for establishing a clear and progressive narrative, guiding the water sector from the early exploration of digital concepts to the practical, widespread application of transformative solutions. The evolution of the Summit’s core themes during this period illustrates a clear maturation of the industry’s digital journey. Establishing the foundations (2022): Defining the core technologies The inaugural Summit, in 2022, focused on defining the digital landscape and exploring the foundational technologies poised to reshape the sector. The agenda was rich with discussions on core concepts such as the Internet of Things (IoT), remote sensing, geographic information systems (GIS) and advanced data analytics. This edition laid the groundwork for future conversations by introducing crucial innovations such as artificial intelligence (AI) and the initial, powerful concept of digital twins for modelling water systems. Driving tangible impact (2023): From theory to application The second summit shifted the conversation towards real-world impact and the role of digital tools in achieving sustainability. The programme featured practical case studies, including leveraging AI for sewer CCTV inspections, the use of Earth Observation techniques for monitoring, and explorations into the potential for digital transformation to improve the sustainability of the water sector. Scaling for global impact (2024): Addressing system-level challenges The most recent edition of the Summit broadened its scope to address system-level challenges and global equity. Discussions centred on achieving net zero emissions through digital tools, developing practical 66 The Summit is uniquely designed as a business-focused ecosystem strategies to benefit lower-middle income countries, and leveraging integrated data to drive systematic change across the entire water sector. This marked a significant step towards a holistic, globally minded approach to digital water. Anew horizon: Istanbul - the perfect host for 2026 The momentum built in Bilbao has set the stage for the Summit’s next evolutionary step, which will unfold in a new international hub. The move to Istanbul for the 2026 edition represents a strategic expansion of the Summit’s global reach and influence. Bringing this critical conversation to a dynamic new crossroads of technology, innovation and culture, signifies a deliberate effort to engage with new regions and foster a more diverse, global dialogue on the future of water. This next chapter in the Summit’s journey will unfold from 7-9 April 2026, at the Halic Congress Center in Istanbul, Tiirkiye. This pivotal event is co-organised by IWA and Istanbul Technical University. By convening in Istanbul, the Digital Water Summit opens its doors to fresh perspectives, new challenges and expanded opportunities for international collaboration, ensuring the agenda remains consistently excellent and refreshingly forward-thinking. What to expect from the 2026 Digital Water Summit As the digital water sector pivots from foundational exploration to scaled implementation, the 2026 Summit agenda is strategically engineered to meet this new frontier. It builds upon the Bilbao legacy not just by continuing the conversation, but by escalating it, informed by direct feedback from the global water community and designed to deliver maximum value, insight, and opportunities for all participants to connect. A proven format for excellence The Summit will retain its highly successful format, which has been praised by attendees. The programme will feature high-impact technical sessions, the InnoHub solutions showcase, interactive panel discussions, and social events designed for high-value networking. A responsive and forward-looking agenda The thematic focus for 2026 has been curated to address the most pressing issues in the digital water space. Core themes will include IoT in water systems, predictive analytics and AI, smart metering, leakage detection, network digital twins, and cybersecurity. This agenda reflects the needs articulated by the global water community in the 2024 feedback survey, which called for deeper dives into AI applications, return on investment analysis for new technologies, and practical case studies on scaling from pilot to full implementation. A business-focused ecosystem The Summit's core purpose is to accelerate the adoption of digital solutions by connecting technology providers and innovators with the utilities and end-users who need them most. It facilitates the critical journey from pilot projects to scaled, system-wide implementation, making it an essential platform for driving tangible progress in the sector.
