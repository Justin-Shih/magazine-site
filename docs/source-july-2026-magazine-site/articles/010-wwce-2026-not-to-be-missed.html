中文全文翻譯

# 2026 世界水大會暨展覽：不容錯過的活動 國際水協會（IWA）的旗艦全球平台，也就是涵蓋整個水循環的世界級活動，將於今年稍晚來到格拉斯哥。大會總監 Kizito Masinde 在本文中展望這場即將登場的盛會。 我們非常高興今年能把世界水大會暨展覽（World Water Congress & Exhibition, WWCE）帶到英國。這項活動每兩年舉辦一次，匯聚來自全球各地的水務專業人士、決策者、研究人員、技術提供者與實務工作者。2026 年格拉斯哥場次將於 10 月 4 日至 8 日舉行，預計會成為我們至今規模最大的一次聚會。 這場大會以 IWA 的全球網絡為基礎，並與在地承辦夥伴緊密合作，同時得到英國各界水務與環境相關組織的支持。屆時預計將有約一萬名參與者齊聚格拉斯哥，共同討論水與衛生服務面臨的重大挑戰，以及能讓社會更具韌性與繁榮的解方。 世界水大會暨展覽提供一個獨特平台，讓政策、科學、創新與實務經驗能夠相互連結。與會者將能接觸最新洞見，理解不同地區與不同部門正在推動的變革，也能在全球水務社群中建立新的合作關係。 本屆大會的主題是「水行動：邁向韌性與繁榮之路」。這個主題反映出水對公共健康、社會穩定、經濟發展與環境保護的基礎作用。從氣候調適到包容性成長，水不只是單一部門的議題，而是所有城市、社群與產業能否面向未來的共同條件。 大會技術方案將圍繞六大核心主題展開：水務公用事業管理、廢水與資源回收、飲用水與飲用級再利用、城市尺度規劃與營運、社群溝通與夥伴關係，以及水資源與永續發展。這些主題涵蓋水循環的主要環節，也回應當前水務部門在治理、基礎設施、氣候風險、資源效率與社會參與方面的共同需求。 本屆活動收到超過 1,400 篇摘要，以及超過 200 件工作坊提案，較多倫多上一屆增加 11%。所有投稿都經過嚴謹審查，最後只有約四分之一的摘要能獲選為口頭發表。這意味著與會者將看到高度篩選後的研究與實務成果，內容品質與國際代表性都將相當突出。 大會也將安排高階峰會，聚焦如何加速落實永續發展目標第六項，也就是確保所有人都能獲得水與衛生服務。峰會之外，議程還包括主題演講、專家座談與多個論壇，涵蓋公用事業領導、監管、青年與新興領袖、地下水、自然復育，以及 One Health 等議題。 技術參訪將展示英國水務部門的領導經驗，讓國際參與者能實地了解當地在供水、廢水處理、資源回收、流域管理與氣候韌性方面的做法。這些參訪不只是展示技術，也能讓不同國家的專業人士比較制度、營運模式與政策工具的差異。 除了專業議程，大會也安排多項社交與文化活動，包括格拉斯哥文化之夜與晚宴。這些場合將提供正式會議以外的交流機會，讓研究者、城市代表、公用事業、企業與國際組織能在更開放的情境中建立合作。 展覽部分同樣是本屆大會的重要亮點。預計將有超過 350 個品牌參展，展覽面積達一萬平方公尺，內容包括國家館、創新解決方案、數位工具與水務技術展示。對於尋找新技術、合作夥伴或市場機會的參與者而言，展覽將是掌握全球水務產業動態的重要場域。 IWA Operations Challenge 也將在大會期間舉行。這項競賽讓公用事業團隊在現場控制室模擬中應對真實感十足的環境緊急事件，情境涵蓋清水與廢水管網受到影響時的處置。參賽者必須在壓力下保護供水安全、降低環境衝擊，並展現跨部門協作與營運判斷能力。 本屆展覽也開放商務參觀者免費登錄，讓更多專業人士能進入展區與商務論壇，接觸最新產品、服務與合作機會。這樣的安排有助於擴大大會影響力，使展覽不只服務註冊代表，也成為更廣泛水務產業互動的平台。 整體而言，2026 年世界水大會暨展覽將是一場充滿活力、內容密集且不容錯過的活動。它不只是一場會議，更是一個讓全球水務社群共同設定方向、分享經驗並促成行動的平台。最新議程與報名資訊可參考大會網站：worldwatercongress.org。

WA's flagship global platform - its world-leading event covering the entire water cycle - comes to Glasgow later this year. Congress Director Kizito Masinde looks ahead to this exceptional event. e are delighted to be bringing the World Water Congress & Exhibition (WWCE) to the UK this year. It is an exciting milestone for the global water community and — as the leading event covering the entire water cycle — the 2026 edition being held in Glasgow from 4-8 October is set to be our largest gathering yet. This ambition is powered by IWA’s global reach and an exceptional partnership with our local hosts which has benefitted from wide involvement and support from across the UK. Together, we look forward to welcoming some 10,000 participants from around the world this October. Renowned for its high-quality content, the Congress offers a vibrant platform for sharing cutting edge insights across policy, science, innovation and practice. The programme will feature a high-level summit to advance action and the agenda on water and sanitation. Alongside this, more than seven forums will explore topics ranging from utility leadership to One Health, complemented by technical sessions and workshops spanning six core Congress themes. The event will also celebrate excellence across the sector, recognising individuals, organisations and projects that are advancing progress in water and sanitation through IWA’s Awards. Above all, the Congress is where the global water community comes together. It is a unique space to connect with utilities, researchers, policymakers and innovators, fostering collaboration, exchanging knowledge and accelerating action towards a more sustainable and resilient water future. Amy Pruden © Peter Means Zhiguo Yuan This year’s Congress theme is ‘Water Action — the Path to Resilience and Prosperity’. This reflects a simple but powerful truth - that water underpins the health, stability and prosperity of our communities, economies and the environment. From climate resilience to inclusive growth, water is central. The technical programme is structured around six core themes that span the full breadth of the water sector: Water Utility Management; Wastewater and Resource Recovery; Drinking Water and Potable Reuse; City-scale Planning and Operations; Communities, Communication and Partnership; and Water Resources and Sustainable Development. Together, these themes create a comprehensive framework for exploring the challenges and opportunities shaping the future of water. The strong global interest in the Congress has long been clear. The call for submissions attracted more than 1400 abstracts and more than 200 workshop proposals — an impressive increase of 11% from the previous Congress in Toronto, Canada. Following a rigorous review process by an international panel of experts, the programme committee carefully curated the final selection into the various categories of oral presentations, poster pitches, posters.and workshops. With such a high level of competition, only one in four abstracts has been selected for oral presentation, ensuring a programme of exceptional quality and relevance. The Congress experience extends well beyond the conference rooms. Delegates will have the opportunity to go on technical tours that showcase the UK’s leadership in the water sector, offering a closer look at local innovation and expertise. Social and cultural moments are woven throughout the week, including a special cultural evening event in Glasgow that blends networking with the city’s vibrant cultural scene. The event will culminate in a gala dinner, providing an opportunity for the global water community to come together, reflect on the week’s insights and celebrate shared achievements over local cuisine and warm hospitality. Running alongside the programme is a dynamic exhibition featuring more than 350 brands across an expansive 10,000 square metres. This lively hub will spotlight cutting-edge technologies, solutions and services, featuring numerous country pavilions as well as others highlighting innovation and digital solutions, creating valuable opportunities for exhibitors and sponsors to connect with leading water professionals. A standout feature on the exhibition floor is the IWA Operations Challenge, where utility teams are tasked with putting their skills to the test in a live control room simulation. Faced with a realistic environmental emergency affecting both clean and wastewater networks, participants will be challenged to protect customer supplies while simultaneously attempting to minimise environmental impact, demonstrating expertise under pressure. As in previous editions, trade visitor registration remains free, offering access to the exhibition and business forum sessions hosted on the show floor - ensuring that even more participants can engage with the innovation, ideas, and connections that define the Congress. IWA’s World Water Congress & Exhibition 2026 is set to be a dynamic, must attend event. We look forward to joining you there to continue the progress that is transforming the water sector.
