中文全文翻譯

# MMIT：強化全球水領域參與 Zafar Razykov 與 Dalerjon Hojiboev 說明，塔吉克礦冶學院（Mining-Metallurgical Institute of Tajikistan, MMIT）如何在慶祝建校 20 週年之際，透過全球合作支持水管理的進展。 2026 年 2 月，塔吉克礦冶學院正式在 UNITWIN／UNESCO Chairs Programme 架構下，成立「水研究、環境教育與永續發展」UNESCO 教席。 這項倡議旨在強化水資源與永續性領域的國際學術合作，同時提升 MMIT 的研究能力，並促進塔吉克的環境教育。相關協議由 UNESCO 總幹事簽署，並由 MMIT 校長 Bakhtiyor Nabi Mahmadali 教授會簽，標誌著中亞水研究與高等教育發展的一項重要里程碑。 同一時期，MMIT 也加入 Erasmus+ GreenCA 計畫。GreenCA 的全名是「Greening Water Education for Sustainable Development in Central Asia」，執行期間為 2026 至 2028 年。這是一項區域性倡議，目的在於現代化與水相關的高等教育，並把綠色與循環經濟原則納入中亞地區的碩士課程。 這兩項進展共同顯示，MMIT 在 2026 年慶祝成立 20 週年之際，正向前邁出重要一步。 ## 推進研究、教育與水治理 新成立的 UNESCO 教席，目標是推動一套整合性的研究、教學、培訓與社群參與系統，聚焦於水資源管理、水質、生態系保育與氣候變遷衝擊。它的設立反映出，在面臨跨境水挑戰、冰川退縮、工業污染風險與氣候引發的水文變異地區，跨學科水治理方法的重要性正在上升。 這項教席也與國家優先事項及國際框架相互呼應，包括聯合國「水促進永續發展」國際行動十年。 MMIT 的 UNESCO 教席設計目標包括：提升水質評估、水化學、地下水保護與跨境水污染方面的科學研究能力；發展創新的教育課程與專業訓練課程；強化國際學術合作；並為以證據為基礎的政策制定與永續水管理作出貢獻。 教席預計每年至少支持五篇同儕審查出版品，開發水管理與環境永續的新課程，並促進與全球研究夥伴的合作。 ## 把綠色轉型嵌入水教育 MMIT 參與 GreenCA 具有特殊意義。GreenCA 匯集歐洲與中亞的 10 所機構，共同回應水壓力、氣候變遷衝擊，以及水務部門降低溫室氣體排放的需求。該計畫由 IWA 董事會成員、挪威生命科學大學（NMBU）的 Harsha Ratnaweera 教授協調。Ratnaweera 教授長期投入中亞事務，對形塑該區域的學術合作具有關鍵作用。 在 GreenCA 架構下，將導入三個符合歐盟標準的碩士模組：資源管理中的數位工具、降低能源使用與溫室氣體排放的最佳化方法，以及資源回收與再利用。 這項計畫的一個鮮明特色，是將在中亞建立六座數位與試點實驗室。這些實驗室整合分析基礎設施、數位工具與模擬式學習，使學生能在實務情境中測試低碳且資源效率高的解方。到 2028 年，該計畫目標是培訓超過 200 名學生，讓課程模組取得歐洲標準認證，並深化與歐洲高等教育區的接軌。 ## 建立在二十年水合作基礎上 這些新倡議建立在 MMIT 自 2006 年成立以來的 20 年發展基礎上。過去十年間，MMIT 參與多項重要的國際水相關計畫，包括針對 Syr Darya 流域跨境水污染的 Science for Peace 計畫。Syr Darya 是橫跨吉爾吉斯、烏茲別克、塔吉克與哈薩克的中亞流域。MMIT 也參與由挪威資助的 Water Harmony 與 Water Harmony Eurasia 倡議。 這些合作促成一座現代化分析實驗室的建立，實驗室配備先進儀器，可用於水質監測與水化學分析。 在教育成果方面，一項重要產出是出版了一本關於水資源管理與水處理方法的綜合教科書，篇幅約 700 頁。這本書由歐洲與中亞學者共同編寫，並以六種語言出版。超過 20 名 MMIT 學生與教職員參與了在挪威的學術流動與培訓活動，強化了教學能力，也拓展了國際視野。此外，相關合作也促成超過 40 篇科學文章發表、博士論文完成答辯，並舉辦多場區域工作坊與會議。 自 2018 年起，MMIT 成為 IWA 的機構會員。學院代表也參與 2019 年在斯里蘭卡可倫坡舉行的 IWA Water and Development Congress & Exhibition，並在其中投入有關氣候韌性與永續水管理的對話。塔吉克在全球水外交中的領導角色，包括推動聯合國水相關決議與「水促進永續發展」國際行動十年，也進一步強化了這些努力的重要性。 ## 展望未來 UNESCO 教席的成立，以及 MMIT 參與 GreenCA，不只是機構層面的成就，也代表一項策略性轉型：從強化實驗室與調和課程，走向推進水教育中的創新、數位化與綠色轉型。 當 MMIT 迎來 20 週年時，該校期待能為這些倡議的成功作出貢獻，並進一步強化與全球社群的合作，共同支持中亞地區的永續水管理。 作者 Zafar Razykov 是塔吉克礦冶學院生態學教授，以及「水研究、環境教育與永續發展」UNESCO 教席主持人。Dalerjon Hojiboev 是塔吉克礦冶學院生態學系主任，以及同一 UNESCO 教席的共同主持人。

Governance MMIT: Strengthening global water engagement Zafar Razykov and Dalerjon Hojiboev describe how the Mining-Metallurgical Institute of Tajikistan (MMIT) is engaging in global collaboration to support advances in water management as it celebrates the institute’s 20th anniversary. n February 2026, the Mining-Metallurgical Institute of Tajikistan (MMIT) officially established the UNESCO Chair on Water Research, Environmental Education, and Sustainable Development under the UNITWIN/ UNESCO Chairs Programme. ; Aimed at bolstering international academic cooperation in water resources and sustainability, this initiative strengthens MMIT’s research capabilities and promotes environmental education in Tajikistan. The Agreement was signed by the Director-General of UNESCO and 7 countersigned by the Rector of MMIT, Professor Bakhtiyor Nabi Mahmadali, marking a significant milestone for water research and higher education development in Central Asia. At the same time, MMIT has joined the Erasmus+ GreenCA project — Greening Water Education for Sustainable Development in Central Asia (2026-2028), a regional initiative aimed at modernising water-related higher education and embedding green and circular economy principles into MSc programmes across Central Asia. ‘ ‘ Together, these two developments signal a major step forward for MMIT as it celebrates its 20th anniversary in 2026. Advancing research, education and water governance The newly established UNESCO Chair aims to promote an integrated system of research, teaching, training and community engagement focused on water resources management, water quality, ecosystem conservation and climate change impacts. Its creation reflects the growing importance of interdisciplinary approaches to water governance in regions facing transboundary water challenges, glacier retreat, industrial pollution risks and climate-induced hydrological variability. The Chair aligns with national priorities and international frameworks, including the UN International Decade for Action “Water Representatives of the Mining-metallurgical Institute of Tajikistan (MMIT) receiving a certificate of membership during the IWA Water and Development Congress & Exhibition 2019 in Colombo, Sri Lanka. © Zafar Razykov/MMIT for Sustainable Development’. The UNESCO Chair at MMIT is designed to: @ Enhance scientific research capacity in water quality assessment, hydrochemistry, groundwater protection and transboundary water pollution; @ Develop innovative educational programmes and professional training courses; ; @ Strengthen international academic cooperation; @ Contribute to evidence-based policymaking and sustainable water management. The Chair will support at least five peer-reviewed publications annually, develop new courses in water management and environmental sustainability, and foster collaboration with global research partners. Embedding the green transition into water education MMIT’s participation in GreenCA has special significance. GreenCA unites 10 institutions from Europe and Central Asia to respond to water stress, the impacts of climate change and the need to reduce greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions in the water sector. The project is coordinated by IWA Board member Professor Harsha Ratnaweera of the Norwegian University of Life Sciences (NMBU), whose long engagement in Central Asia has been key to shaping academic cooperation in the region.Under GreenCA, three EU-certified MSc modules will be implemented: © Digital tools in resource management © Optimisation to reduce energy use and GHG emissions @ Resource recovery and reuse A distinctive feature of the project is the establishment of six digital and pilot laboratories across Central Asia. These labs integrate analytical infrastructure with digital tools and simulation-based learning, enabling students to test low-carbon and resource-efficient solutions in practical contexts. By 2028, the project aims to train more than 200 students, certify modules under European standards and deepen alignment with the European Higher Education Area. aN NMI Mining-metallurgical Institute of Tajikistan (MMIT), Buston, Tajikistan. © Dalerjon Hojiboev/MMIT Governance Building on two decades of water cooperation These new initiatives build upon MMIT’s 20-year development since its establishment in 2006. Over the past decade, MMIT has participated in major international water-related projects, including the Science for Peace programme on transboundary water pollution in the Syr Darya basin (a Central Asian watershed spanning Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, and Kazakhstan) and the Norwegian-funded Water Harmony and Water Harmony Eurasia initiatives. These collaborations enabled the establishment of a modern analytical laboratory equipped with advanced instruments for water quality monitoring and hydrochemical analysis. Akey educational outcome was the publication of'a comprehensive textbook on water resource management and water treatment methods (approximately 700 pages), prepared jointly by European and Central Asian scholars and published in six languages. More than 20 students and staff from MMIT participated in academic mobility and training activities in Norway, strengthening pedagogical capacity and international exposure. More than 40 scientific articles were published, PhD theses defended, and numerous regional workshops and conferences were organised. Since 2018, MMIT has been an institutional member of IWA, and institute representatives participated in TWA’s Water and Development Congress & Exhibition 2019 in Colombo, Sri Lanka, contributing to dialogue on climate- resilient and sustainable water management. Tajikistan’s leadership in global water diplomacy, including UN resolutions on water and the International Decade for Action ‘Water for Sustainable Development’, further reinforces the relevance of these efforts. Looking ahead The establishment of the UNESCO Chair and participation in GreenCA represent not only institutional achievements but a strategic transition — from strengthening laboratories and harmonising curricula towards advancing innovation, digitalisation and green transformation in water education.
