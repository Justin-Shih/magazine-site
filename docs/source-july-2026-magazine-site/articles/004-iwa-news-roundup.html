﻿## IWA 新聞 ### 最新一批水專業人士接受 LeaP 挑戰 IWA 新一輪 LeaP Leadership Programme for Young Water Professionals（YWP）正在展開；與此同時，第一屆學員也將在 10 月於英國格拉斯哥舉行的 IWA World Water Conference & Exhibition 工作坊中，完成他們的活動。 IWA LeaP 的每一輪都讓 12 位來自不同背景與地區的優秀青年水專業人士迎接領導力挑戰。在 12 個月期間，參與者建立穩固的專業網絡，並在實現領導潛能的過程中強化自己的領導技能。 ### 水安全會議代表確認對韌性水系統的承諾 第七屆 IWA Water Safety Conference 於 2026 年 3 月 23 至 25 日在斯里蘭卡 Colombo 舉行，匯聚多元的全球水專業社群，推進關於水安全與韌性的對話和行動。 這場會議由 National Water Supply and Drainage Board（NWSDB）主辦，迎來 20 多個國家的代表，包括公用事業、研究者、政策制定者、國際組織與青年水專業人士。會議也凸顯斯里蘭卡在最高政治層級上對水安全的承諾。 IWA 執行主任 Kala Vairavamoorthy 在開幕主題演講〈Rethinking Water: Building Safe, Circular and Resilient Systems〉中強調，水部門需要超越以合規為主的做法。他呼籲將水安全規劃內化為一種整體思維，重點在於端到端理解系統、預判風險，並在整個水循環中主動管理風險。 ### 丹麥青年水專業人士交流實作經驗與技術洞察 IWA Young Water Professionals Denmark Chapter 於 3 月舉辦第 11 屆全國會議，集合丹麥水部門各地的青年水專業人士，進行兩天的學習、合作與對話。 會議在 Aarhus 市多個場地舉行，結合技術洞察、實作經驗，以及關於水部門未來的討論。第一天在 Aarhus University 舉行，主題聚焦水韌性與跨領域知識交流。當天以簡報和討論開場，凸顯與韌性水系統相關的當前研究與挑戰。 接著是青年水專業人士的技術簡報，展示來自公用事業、顧問和研究領域的多種專案、觀點與經驗。活動也包括地質建模工作坊，互動形式鼓勵合作，並把理論知識與實務應用連接起來。 第一天以社交晚宴作結，並頒發獎項，表揚 YWP 的參與、領導力與傑出貢獻。第二天，參與者聚集於 DANVA，也就是丹麥水與廢水協會，焦點從技術議題轉向水部門的人才與組織面向。 議程的核心之一，是與資深公用事業專業人士共同舉辦工作坊，討論水部門如何更好地留住青年水專業人士，並強化跨世代合作。活動最後，參與者參訪當地水公司 Aarhus Vand，進行以漏水管理為主題的導覽。 ### 產業領袖、研究者與青年水專業人士在 IWA 英國兩日峰會交流想法 2026 年 IWA UK Young Water Professionals Conference 於 3 月在 Birmingham 舉行，匯聚水部門各界人士，包括研究者、產業領袖與職涯早期專業人士，讓他們在同一場域分享想法、經驗，以及對水未來的觀點。 議程為期兩天，討論主題從英國 Lake Windermere 的水質，到奈及利亞的乾旱預測都有涵蓋。在正式議程之外，與會者也圍繞職涯路徑，以及水部門面臨的挑戰與可能性進行交流。 除了會議議程，代表們也有機會探索 Birmingham Aquarium 的幕後空間，參觀 Dudley 的地下隧道系統，並參加拉丁風格的晚宴。

IWA News Latest cohort of water professionals take LeaP challenge The new round of WA's LeaP Leadership Programme for Young Water Professionals (YWPs) is getting under way, alongside which the first cohort of the programme is set to conclude their activities with a workshop at IWA’s World Water Conference & Exhibition in Glasgow, UK, in October. Each round of IWA LeaP enables 12 exceptional YWPs from diverse backgrounds and regions to rise to the leadership challenge. Over 12 months, participants forge robust professional networks and enhance their leadership skills on their journey to fulfil their leadership potential. Safety conference delegates confirm commitment to resilient water systems The 7th IWA Water Safety Conference held from 23-25 March 2026 in Colombo, Sri Lanka, brought together a diverse global community of water professionals to advance dialogue and action on water safety and resilience. The conference, organised by the National Water Supply and Drainage Board (NWSDB), welcomed delegates from more than 20 countries, including utilities, researchers, policymakers, international organisations and Young Water Professionals (YWPs), with the conference underscoring Sri Lanka’s commitment to water safety at the highest political level. In his opening keynote speech, ‘Rethinking Water: Building Safe, Circular and Resilient Systems’, |WA Executive Director Kala Vairavamoorthy emphasised the need to move beyond compliance-based approaches. He called for water safety planning to be embedded as a holistic mindset, focused onunderstanding systems end to end, anticipating risks and managing them proactively across the water cycle. Hands-on experience and tech insights as Danish YWPs meet The IWA Young Water Professionals (YWP) Denmark Chapter held its 11th national conference in March, bringing together YWPs from across the Danish water sector for two days of learning, collaboration and dialogue. The conference, hosted in venues across the city of Aarhus, combined technical insight, hands-on experiences and discussions on the future of the sector. The first day of the conference took place at Aarhus University, where the focus was on water resilience and knowledge exchange across disciplines. The day opened with presentations and discussions highlighting current research and challenges: related to resilient water systems. This was followed by technical presentations from YWPs, showcasing a wide range of projects, perspectives and experiences from across utilities, consulting and research. Activities included a workshop on geological modelling, with the interactive format of the event encouraging collaboration, bridging theoretical knowledge with practical application. The first day concluded with a social dinner, with awards presented to celebrate YWP engagement, leadership and outstanding contributions. On the second day, participants gathered at DANVA (the Danish Water and Wastewater Association) where the focus shifted from technical topics to the human and organisational side of the water sector. Acentral element of the programme was a joint workshop with senior utility professionals addressing how the sector can better retain YWPs and strengthen collaboration across generations. Closing the event, participants visited local water company Aarhus Vand for a guided tour focused on leakage management. The programme took place across two days, with sessions covering topics ranging from water quality in the UK’s Lake Windermere to drought prediction in Nigeria. Outside the formal agenda, discussions took place around career paths and the challenges and possibilities for the water sector. Alongside the conference programme, delegates were able to explore behind the scenes at the Birmingham Aquarium, visit the underground tunnel systems of Dudley, and enjoy a Latin-inspired gala dinner. Systemic characteristics of flood disasters in Hong Kong Frequent extreme rainfall with intensified hazards Hong Kong is among the world’s wettest cities, with an annual rainfall of about 2400 mm driven by monsoons and tropical cyclones. Rainfall is concentrated between May and September, with events exceeding 100 mm occurring over three times annually. These storms exhibit sudden onset, spatial variability and short warning windows. For example, hourly rainfall exceeded 150 mm on 7 September 2023 and 100 mm in the North District in August 2025, causing widespread traffic paralysis. Such extreme rainfall necessitates reliance on forecasting accuracy and rapid response.
