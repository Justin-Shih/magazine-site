# 解決方案：水務基礎設施 ## 處理技術：推出新的行動式奈米過濾單元 Saur Group 旗下的 Nijhuis Saur Industries（NSI）與 Nx Filtration（NXF）推出行動式奈米過濾單元 MONF。此系統結合 NSI 在工業與市政水處理的專業，以及 NXF 的中空纖維膜技術，提供模組化、無需化學藥劑的解決方案，讓產業得以有效地閉合水循環。 廠商表示，其主要特色包括可有效去除微污染物、硫酸鹽與有機物，降低營運成本與水足跡，以及即插即用的模組化設計。 製造商稱，MONF 每小時最高可處理 85 立方公尺水量，適合尋求成本有效且可調適水處理選項的企業。 ## 循環經濟：AI 公司將再利用廢水供資料中心冷卻 人工智慧（AI）公司 xAI 將興建全球規模最大的陶瓷膜生物反應器（MBR）之一，每日可將 4,920 萬公升市政廢水再利用，作為美國孟菲斯超級運算資料中心的冷卻水。Cerafiltec 將在一項預定於 2025 年完成的快速推進計畫中，提供先進陶瓷膜技術。 此 MBR 計畫的龐大規模，凸顯資料中心冷卻等關鍵廢水處理應用對先進陶瓷解決方案日益增加的需求。在迅速擴張的資料中心產業中，有效且永續的水管理，是維持高營運效能不可或缺的條件。 xAI 表示，該計畫以市政廢水進行冷卻，藉此保存飲用水、保護孟菲斯砂層含水層，使當地社區受益，彰顯公司對永續水管理的承諾。 這座廠的廢水處理能力超過 xAI 自身需求，因而也會向當地產業提供多餘的處理水，減少它們對含水層的影響，並鼓勵更多高科技產業以循環經濟的方式永續營運。 ## 數位化：義大利供水業者推行智慧計量 義大利供水業者 Publiacqua 正在托斯卡尼地區使用 Itron 的超音波水表，以減少漏水並改善與客戶的互動。 每一只 Itron Intelis wSource 水表都配備先進的漏水偵測技術，確保蒐集到的資料可靠且精準。 隨著公用事業轉用 Itron 智慧水表，Publiacqua 將運用智慧系統的即時資料蒐集、帳務精確性、流量與配水監測、客戶警示，以及偵測異常時產生警報等功能。 ## 循環經濟：推出氣候中和塑膠污水系統 高分子材料專家 REHAU 推出首套百分之百氣候中和塑膠污水系統 nevoPP。公司稱，該系統在整個產品生命週期中的氣候影響都可完整量測。這套新的永續污水管系統著重以循環經濟達成減排，採用以高品質聚丙烯製成、百分之百再生的高分子材料。 製造商指出，與完全使用原生材料建造的系統相比，鋪設 50 公里這種污水管系統，潛在可減少約 360 公噸二氧化碳排放；相當於約 75 戶英國一般家庭一年的二氧化碳排放量。 此一減排量已由領先的測試、檢驗與認證服務機構 TÜV Rheinland 獨立計算並驗證。

Solutions Treatment New mobile nanofiltration unit launched Nijhuis Saur Industries (NSI), part of Saur Group, and Nx Filtration (NXF) have launched the mobile nanofiltration unit MONF. This merges NSI’s expertise in industrial and municipal water treatment with NXF’s hollow fibre membrane technology, to provide a modular and chemical-free solution that allows industries to effectively close the water loop. Key features are said to include the effective removal of micropollutants, sulphates and organics, reduced operational costs and water footprint, and plug-and-play modularity. The makers say MONF can treat up to 85 m‘/h, making it ideal for businesses seeking cost-effective and adaptable water treatment options. Circular economy Al company to reuse wastewater for data centre cooling Artificial Intelligence (Al) company xAl is to build one of the world’s largest ceramic membrane bioreactors (MBR) to enable the reuse of 49.2 megalitres per day (MLD) of municipal wastewater for cooling its supercomputing data centre in Memphis, USA. Cerafiltec will deliver advanced ceramic membrane technology in a fast-track project set for completion in 2025. The enormous scale of this MBR project highlights the growing demand for advanced ceramic solutions in critical applications of wastewater treatment, such as data centre cooling. Ina rapidly expanding data centre industry, effective, sustainable water management is essential to maintain high operational performance. XAl says this project underscores the company’s commitment to sustainable water management by using municipal wastewater for cooling, thereby conserving drinking water and protecting the Memphis Sands Aquifer for the benefit of local communities. With its capacity to treat more wastewater than xAl requires, the plant will also provide surplus treated water to local industries, reducing their impact on the aquifer and encouraging more high-tech industries to operate sustainably as part of the circular economy. Digitalisation Italian water provider rolls out smart metering Italian water provider Publiacqua is using Itron’s ultrasonic water meters to reduce water loss and improve customer engagement in the region of Tuscany. Each Itron Intelis wSource water meter is equipped with advanced leak detection technology, ensuring the data collected is reliable and accurate. As the utility transitions to Itron’s smart water meters, Publiacqua will take advantage of the smart system's real-time data collection, billing accuracy, flow and distribution monitoring, customer alerts, and the generation of alarms when anomalies are detected. Circular economy Climate neutral plastic sewer system launched Polymer specialist REHAU has launched its first 100% climate neutral plastic sewer system, nevoPP, which it says has a completely measurable climate impact throughout the entire product life-cycle. The new sustainable sewage pipe system focuses on using the circular economy to achieve emissions savings and is constructed from a 100% recycled polymer made from high-quality polypropylene. The manufacturer states that the installation of 50 km of this sewer pipe system has the potential to save around 360 tonnes of CO, compared with a system that uses entirely new materials in its construction. This is the equivalent of the annual CO, emissions from approximately 75 average UK homes. This reduction has been independently calculated and verified by TUV Rheinland, a leading provider of testing, inspection and certification.
