# 解決方案：數位化 ## 監測：新應用程式追蹤颶風引發的沿海洪水 美國南佛羅里達大學研究人員使用一款新應用程式，追蹤颶風期間的沿海洪水。 這項網頁式 CRIS-HAZARD 應用程式由該校與喬治亞理工學院共同開發，蒐集群眾外包資料，以辨識洪水並為沿海社區政策提供資訊。它使用動態建模與地圖工具，可由使用者上傳的影像辨識並擷取估算水位。除向研究人員提供資料外，這些照片與影片也可供其他人查看，協助社區掌握危害資訊。 目前，這款應用程式用於分析佛羅里達州皮尼拉斯郡的即時淹水情形；該郡海岸線約 950 公里。研究團隊希望未來能讓全國各地社區使用這項工具。 ## 商業：AI 水務供應商完成 1,400 萬美元募資 提供水與廢水管理人工智慧（AI）及物聯網（IoT）解決方案的 Turing 宣布，已成功完成 1,400 萬美元融資。 這筆資金將加速公司全球擴張，並強化 AI 驅動的平台：為公用事業提供數位解決方案的 TOP Clear，以及服務工業處理的水管理平台 SmartOps AI。公司表示，兩平台可提供即時控制與監測、進階異常偵測、預測分析及智慧決策。 ## 資料管理：VertiGIS 推出新的空間資產管理平台 全球空間資產管理解決方案供應商 VertiGIS 推出 VertiGIS ConnectMaster for ArcGIS，目標是改變公用事業管理管網資產清冊與營運的方式。 ArcGIS 軟體將地理資料與進階分析工具串聯，使使用者能建立、管理與分享資料。VertiGIS 表示，透過在 ConnectMaster 中嵌入 ArcGIS 功能，可提供即時洞見與卓越營運能力，提升管網及資產資料的可取得性、提供具未來擴充性的規模能力、提高使用者生產力，並改善成本效率與資源最佳化。

Solutions - Digital Monitoring New app tracks hurricane coastal flooding Researchers from the University of South Florida, USA, have used a new app to track coastal flooding during hurricanes. The web-based CRIS-HAZARD app, developed with Georgia Institute of Technology, gathers crowdsourced data to identify flooding and inform policy in coastal communities. Using dynamic modelling and mapping tools, it can identify and extract estimated water elevation from submitted imagery. In addition to the data provided for researchers, the pictures and videos are accessible to others to help keep communities informed of hazards. Currently used to analyse real-time flooding in Pinellas County, Florida, which has approximately 950 km of coastline, it is hoped that the app will be made available to communities nationwide. Business Al water sector provider completes $14m funding round Turing, a provider of artificial intelligence (Al) and Internet of Things (loT) solutions: for water and wastewater management, has announced the successful completion of a $14m funding round. This will accelerate the company’s global expansion and enhance Al-powered platforms TOP Clear, providing digital solutions for the utility sector, and SmartOps Al, its water management platform for industrial treatment. The company says these enable real-time control and monitoring, advanced anomaly detection, predictive analytics, and intelligent decision-making. Data management VertiGIS launches new spatial asset management platform VertiGIS, a global provider of spatial asset management solutions, has launched VertiGIS ConnectMaster for ArcGIS, to transform how utilities manage their network inventory and operations. ArcGIS software connects geographical data with advanced analytical tools, enabling users to create, manage and share data. By embedding ArcGIS functionality within ConnectMaster, VertiGIS says it can deliver real-time insights and operational excellence, enhancing network and asset data accessibility, future-proofing scalability, enhancing user productivity, and improving cost efficiency and resource optimisation.
