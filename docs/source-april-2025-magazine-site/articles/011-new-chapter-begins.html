# 新篇章啟動 IWA 未來數年的企圖心，已載入其《2025–2030 策略計畫》。Kala Vairavamoorthy 說明即將到來的轉型機會，以及會員如何參與這段重要旅程。 策略計畫的核心是一項大膽的願景行動──它是我們走過之處與下一步敢於前往之地的橋梁。隨著 IWA 新的《2025–2030 策略計畫》發布，我們不只是繼續旅程，而是開啟充滿活力的新篇章；它由企圖心塑造，並由目標引導。 這份計畫建立在堅實而值得自豪的基礎上。逾 75 年來，IWA 一直是全球領導者，動力來自會員的熱情與投入。我們上一份 2019 至 2024 年策略計畫取得顯著成果：會員數成長 26%；我們迎來 14 個新理事會員，使領導社群由 51 個擴大至 65 個；青年水專業人士（YWP）分會數量增加逾一倍，由 22 個增至 50 個，為下一代領導者創造新的茁壯空間。 我們也擴大全球足跡，顯著強化在亞洲、非洲與拉丁美洲的能見度。在過程中，我們推出大膽且具意義的倡議，從數位水、氣候智慧公用事業，到包容性都市衛生。這些成功並非偶然，而是由會員──也就是你們──以及緊密連結、協作網絡的力量所推動。 IWA 的獨特之處在於凝聚人群的方式。我們靈活、開放且深度協作。這種精神存在於以多元、公平與共融為根基的文化中；每一個跨越性別、世代與區域的聲音，都有清楚路徑可在協會中承擔領導角色。現在，我們將帶著更新的焦點與共同目標，將這種精神帶往準備共同塑造的未來。 ## 從願景到行動 這份新策略計畫在 IWA 與全球水務部門的關鍵時刻推出。2030 年的期限迫在眉睫，特別是它是達成聯合國永續發展目標的截止點。這份急迫性反映在新計畫中；它已於去年加拿大多倫多世界水大會暨展覽期間，獲理事大會正式支持。 重要的是，這份計畫不只是一個願景，而是一項行動呼籲。為將企圖化為行動，IWA 秘書處制定詳細營運計畫，說明我們將如何交付策略。董事會於二月審查並一致通過這份路徑圖，反映其強力支持與共同承諾。它現在提供清楚架構，指引秘書處工作並界定前路；但成功仍取決於會員的活力、參與及領導。會員參與不只是受到歡迎，而是不可或缺；我們將共同使這項策略落實。 這次有何不同？在許多方面，我們的核心認同未變──仍是致力於永續水解決方案的全球網絡──但企圖心規模顯著成長。我們更聚焦氣候行動、公平、數位創新與跨部門協作，並透過 23 項具體策略目標來推動；這些目標不只反映世界發展方向，也反映 IWA 可在哪裡領導。 ## 引領改變 先從一項重大企圖談起：將 IWA 建立為水與衛生公用事業的全球家園。這遠不只是參與，而是切合需求。我們將在各屆大會設置公用事業專屬規劃、推出全球水務公用事業高峰會，並為面對當今營運及氣候相關挑戰的公用事業，開發實務支援。新的具認證培訓與證照計畫，將使專業人士具備茁壯所需技能。這些行動建立於氣候智慧公用事業等倡議所創造的動能，強化我們對全產業轉型的承諾。 同時，我們的全球衛生倡議旨在提升衛生服務在部門內及全球對話中的地位。透過以科學為基礎的知識中心、新的全球衛生高峰會與目標導向倡議，我們將使衛生服務回到應有的位置：最核心的位置。我們也正協助塑造 2030 年後的水與衛生議程，確保永續發展目標（SDG）6.2 仍是全球優先事項。 我們也高度聚焦於支撐網絡的人。對全世界的理事會員，我們將推出章程、新參與工具與表揚計畫，展現積極會員及其支持的地方倡議。新的數位儀表板與年度行動計畫，將支持共同問責並追蹤集體進展。我們也會推出聚焦拓展行動，歡迎更多來自目前代表不足地區的理事會員。 公平、多元與共融仍是工作的核心。我們將強化 IWA 女性領導網絡，使協會中的女性在每項計畫中──從衛生到數位水──獲得更多能見度、導師支持與領導機會。 對 YWPs，我們將加倍投入，擴大導師制度、領導路徑及對 YWP 分會的支持，特別是代表不足區域。LEaP 領導力計畫與 SDG 6 青年行動獎助計畫等倡議只是開始；新的數位追蹤平台將協助 YWP 分會監測進展、分享學習並保持連結。 另一個令人振奮的前線是跨部門影響力。水與一切相連──農業、能源、產業──我們準備擴展影響範圍。一項新倡議將與關鍵產業建立夥伴關係，分享 IWA 在再利用、低碳處理、資源回收與數位最佳化的專業，以推動跨部門永續性。 ## 更強、更智慧的網絡 若要作為網絡而蓬勃發展，我們需要更緊密連結、更具包容性，並更受到數位工具賦能。因此，我們正透過更智慧的工具與更有意義的體驗，轉型會員參與。 我們的 Connect Plus 平台正演進為互動樞紐，會員可透過全球地圖、可搜尋名錄與即時訊息彼此連結。新的虛擬交流活動、數位競賽與聚焦的微型社群，將使會員圍繞共同興趣與區域優先事項相聚。 一項突出倡議是數位大使計畫，旨在賦能新興領導者，特別是面臨實體參與障礙者。透過提供數位參與、內容創作與個人品牌培訓，我們將協助這些大使建立影響力、主持虛擬活動，並跨平台代表 IWA 使命。他們的聲音與故事將透過 IWA 管道獲得提升，協助形塑更具包容性、全球連結更緊密的水務社群。 同樣重要的是，我們如何認可貢獻。無論是擔任導師、分享知識或帶領討論，會員都將透過焦點報導、數位代幣及 IWA 平台上的表揚而受到肯定。遊戲化體驗將提供點數、徽章與展現專業參與的新方式。 ## 創新我們的學習與會議方式 知識是 IWA 的核心，而我們正使這個核心面向大幅躍進。AI 將協助我們使洞見更容易取得，提供多語翻譯、清晰且針對特定受眾的摘要，以及為政策制定者、操作人員、學生及其他人量身打造的互動格式。 我們的會議正被重新想像為智慧活動。AI 將支持議程設計、減少內容重疊，並提供個人化場次建議。即時回饋工具將使我們能即時調整並延長高關注度場次，使每個人的體驗更切合需求且更具動態。 沒錯，《The Source》也將進行數位更新。我們的旗艦出版品將提供多媒體摘要、量身打造的內容與 AI 驅動工具，使會員能以多種語言、依照自身興趣，輕鬆按需取得內容。 ## 將策略化為行動 偉大的計畫，其力量取決於實現它的人。雖然秘書處已全力交付這份路徑圖，真正動能將來自會員，在網絡每個角落將企圖化為行動。 這項策略由會員的洞見、活力與想法所形塑。世界各地的理事會員、專業小組、YWPs 與夥伴皆參與其發展。現在，同樣的共同領導精神必須帶領我們前進。 因此，這是我們的邀請：請參與。無論是帶領地方活動、指導未來領導者、為專業小組作出貢獻，或在所在區域放大我們的聲音──你的行動都將形塑我們共同創造的影響。 每一種貢獻都有空間，且此刻正是投入的最佳時機。這份策略計畫是具企圖心的意向聲明，關乎我們如何領導、如何連結，以及如何共同形塑未來。接下來將由我們的集體承諾所定義──由我們如何出現、分享知識、跨境協作，並持續將公平與創新置於所有工作核心所定義。 因此，我邀請你投入，成為下一步的一部分。憑藉你的活力、聲音與領導力，我相信我們能夠完成真正具轉型性的事。 我們已準備好了！ 作者 Kala Vairavamoorthy 為國際水協會執行長。 ## IWA《2025–2030 策略計畫》 ### 我們的願景 一個水資源被明智、永續且公平管理的世界。 ### 我們的使命 壯大並賦能由水務及相關專業人士組成的會員網絡，匯聚網絡的活力與洞見，以：發展並傳播知識與良好實務；推動創新、建構能力並培育領導力；以及支持全球社群針對永續發展目標與氣候變遷採取行動，並為 2030 年後議程作準備。 ### 五年後的 IWA──我們的目標 #### 推動改變：團結水永續的聲音 在 IWA 涵蓋的各種水務關注範圍中，維持一個充滿活力、多元的會員群，促進連結、共融、影響、影響力、專業成長及全球水務社群中的共同成功。 #### 提升水務智慧：引領永續行動 鞏固我們作為受信賴權威與全球水知識樞紐的角色，熟練處理並提升大眾對核心供水與衛生需求、更廣泛新興趨勢與議題的認識，並透過開創性的思想領導與具影響力的計畫，在全球倡議永續成果。 #### 賦能解決方案：為全球水務部門量身打造內容 勤勉策劃並提供廣泛的專業內容與計畫，以賦能會員為基礎，並精準量身打造，以滿足全球水務部門不斷演變的需求。 #### 為水創新與領導力發展進行全球協作 運用網絡中全球研究與實務領導者的參與，推動水務部門創新，促進治理與夥伴關係以實現此目標；同時支持強化部門能力與領導力，以促成具體進展。 #### 因應世界水挑戰：引領倡議與協作 我們將強化作為全球社群中水務重要倡議者的定位，優先針對永續發展目標（SDGs）與氣候變遷採取知情行動，同時為 2030 年後議程作準備；我們將營造安全環境，分享水管理知識並慶祝具影響力的協作。

The Source官方網頁原文（查詢日期：2026-09-10）

IWA’s ambitions for the coming years are set out in its Strategic Plan for 2025-2030. Kala Vairavamoorthy highlights the transformative opportunities that await and how members can engage on this important journey At its core, a strategic plan is a bold act of vision – a bridge between where we’ve been and where we dare to go next. With IWA’s new Strategic Plan for 2025–2030, we are not merely continuing our journey; we are opening a dynamic new chapter, shaped by ambition and guided by purpose. We’ve built this plan on a strong and proud foundation. For more than 75 years, IWA has been a global leader, powered by the passion and dedication of its members. Our last Strategic Plan, from 2019 to 2024, delivered remarkable results. Membership grew by 26%. We welcomed 14 new Governing Members, expanding our leadership community from 51 to 65. The number of our Young Water Professional (YWP) chapters more than doubled – from 22 to 50 – creating new spaces for the next generation of leaders to thrive. We also broadened our global footprint, significantly strengthening our presence in Asia, Africa and Latin America. Along the way, we launched bold and meaningful initiatives – from Digital Water and Climate Smart Utilities to Inclusive Urban Sanitation. These successes didn’t happen by chance. They were driven by you – our members – and by the power of our connected, collaborative network. What sets IWA apart is the way we bring people together. We are nimble, open and deeply collaborative. This spirit lives within a culture grounded in diversity, equity and inclusion – where every voice, across genders, generations and regions, has a clear path to leadership in our Association. Now, we carry that spirit forward, with renewed focus and shared purpose, into the future we are ready to shape – together. From vision to action This new Strategic Plan lands at a crucial moment, not just for IWA, but for the global water sector. The year 2030 looms large on the horizon, especially as the deadline for achieving the UN Sustainable Development Goals. That urgency is reflected in our new plan, which was formally endorsed by the Governing Assembly during last year’s World Water Congress & Exhibition in Toronto, Canada. Importantly, this plan is more than a vision – it’s a call to action. To turn ambition into action, the IWA Secretariat has developed a detailed operational plan that sets out how we will deliver on the strategy. This roadmap was reviewed and unanimously approved by the Board in February, reflecting their strong endorsement and shared commitment. It now provides a clear framework that guides the Secretariat’s work and defines the path ahead. But its success depends on the energy, engagement and leadership of you – our members. Your involvement is not just welcomed; it’s essential. Together, we will bring this strategy to life. So, what’s different this time around? In many ways, our core identity hasn’t changed – we’re still a global network committed to sustainable water solutions – but the scale of our ambition has grown significantly. We’ve sharpened our focus on climate action, equity, digital innovation and cross-sector collaboration. And we’ve done so through 23 specific strategic goals that reflect not just where the world is going, but where IWA can lead. Leading the change Let’s start with a major ambition: establishing IWA as the global home for water and sanitation utilities. This is about much more than engagement – it’s about relevance. We’re creating dedicated programming for utilities at our Congresses, launching a Global Water Utilities Summit, and developing practical support for utilities facing today’s operational and climate-related challenges. New credentialed training and certification programmes will equip professionals with the skills they need to thrive. This all builds on the momentum we’ve created through initiatives such as Climate Smart Utilities, reinforcing our commitment to sector-wide transformation. Alongside this, our Global Sanitation Initiative aims to elevate sanitation within the sector – and in the global conversation. Through a science-based Knowledge Hub, a new Global Sanitation Summit and targeted advocacy, we’ll put sanitation where it belongs: front and centre. And we are helping shape the post-2030 water and sanitation agenda, ensuring Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 6.2 remains a global priority. We’re also deeply focused on the people who power our network. For our Governing Members around the world, we’re introducing a Charter, new engagement tools, and a recognition programme that will showcase active members and the local initiatives they champion. New digital dashboards and annual action plans will support shared accountability and track our collective progress. We’ll also launch a focused outreach drive to welcome more Governing Members from currently underrepresented regions. Equity, diversity and inclusion remain at the heart of our work. We’re strengthening the IWA Women’s Leadership Network, giving women across the Association greater opportunities for visibility, mentoring and leadership – across every programme, from sanitation to digital water. And for our YWPs, we’re doubling down – expanding mentorship, leadership pathways and support for YWP Chapters, especially in underrepresented regions. Initiatives such as our LeaP Leadership Programme and the Youth Action for SDG 6 Fellowship are just the beginning. A new digital tracking platform will help YWP Chapters monitor progress, share learning and stay connected. Another exciting frontier is cross-sector influence. Water connects to everything – agriculture, energy, industry – and we’re ready to expand our reach. A new initiative will build partnerships with key industries, sharing IWA’s expertise in reuse, low-carbon treatment, resource recovery and digital optimisation, to drive sustainability across sectors. A stronger, smarter network To thrive as a network, we need to be more connected, inclusive and digitally empowered. That’s why we’re transforming member engagement through smarter tools and more meaningful experiences. Our Connect Plus platform is evolving into an interactive hub where members can connect via a global map, searchable directory and real-time messaging. New virtual networking events, digital competitions and focused micro-communities will bring members together around shared interests and regional priorities. A standout initiative is the Digital Ambassadors Programme, designed to empower emerging leaders – especially those facing barriers to in-person participation. By providing training in digital engagement, content creation and personal branding, we’ll help these ambassadors build influence, lead virtual events and represent IWA’s mission across platforms. Their voices and stories will be elevated through IWA channels, helping shape a more inclusive and globally connected water community. Equally important is how we recognise contributions. Whether mentoring, sharing knowledge or leading discussion, members will be celebrated through spotlight features, digital tokens and recognition across IWA platforms. Gamified experiences will offer points, badges and new ways to showcase professional engagement. Innovating how we learn and meet Knowledge is at the heart of IWA – and we’re taking a leap forward with this core dimension. AI will help us make insights more accessible, with multilingual translations, clear audience-specific summaries, and interactive formats tailored for policymakers, operators, students and others. Our conferences are being reimagined as smart events. AI will support programme design, reduce content overlap and deliver personalised session recommendations. Real-time feedback tools will let us adapt and extend high-interest sessions on the fly, making the experience more relevant and dynamic for everyone. And, yes, The Source is getting a digital refresh. Our flagship publication will feature multimedia summaries, tailored content and AI-powered tools for easy, on-demand access – in multiple languages and aligned to member interests. Turning strategy into action Great plans are only as powerful as the people who bring them to life. While the Secretariat is already hard at work delivering the roadmap, the real momentum will come from you – our members – turning ambition into action across every corner of our network. This strategy was shaped through your insight, energy and ideas. Governing Members, Specialist Groups, YWPs and partners from around the world all contributed to its development. That same spirit of shared leadership now needs to carry us forward. So, this is our invitation: get involved. Whether it’s leading a local activity, mentoring a future leader, contributing to a Specialist Group or amplifying our voice in your region – your action will help shape the impact we make together. There’s space for every kind of contribution. And there’s no better time to step in. This Strategic Plan is an ambitious statement of intent. It’s about how we lead, how we connect, and how we shape the future together. What comes next will be defined by our collective commitment – by how we show up, share knowledge, collaborate across borders, and keep equity and innovation at the centre of all we do. So, I invite you to step in. Be part of what comes next. With your energy, your voice and your leadership, I believe we can do something truly transformative. Together, we are ready! The author: Kala Vairavamoorthy is the CEO of the International Water Association IWA Strategic Plan 2025-2030 Our vision A world in which water is wisely, sustainably and equitably managed. Elevating water wisdom: Leading the charge for sustainability Solidify our role as a trusted authority and global hub for water knowledge, adeptly addressing and raising awareness on core water supply and sanitation needs, of wider emerging trends and issues, and championing sustainable outcomes worldwide through pioneering thought leadership and impactful programmes. Empowering solutions: Tailored content for the global water sector Have diligently curated and delivered an expansive spectrum of professional content and programming, bolstered by empowering our members and precisely tailored to meet the evolving demands of the water sector around the world. Navigating the world’s water challenges: Leading advocacy and collaboration We will strengthen our position as a leading advocate for water within global communities, prioritising informed action on the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and climate change, while also preparing for the post-2030 agenda, by fostering a secure environment for sharing water management knowledge and celebrating impactful collaborations.
