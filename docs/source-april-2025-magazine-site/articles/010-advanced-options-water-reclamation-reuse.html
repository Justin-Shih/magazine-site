中文全文翻譯

# 水回收與再利用的進階選項 在公司迎接創立一百週年之際，印度兩項計畫凸顯水回收與再利用在工業應用上的機會。J. Lahnsteiner 與 BK Kumaran 介紹這些計畫。 WABAG（現為 VA Tech WABAG）成立於 1924 年 8 月，擁有逾一世紀的悠久歷史，提供多樣化的永續水技術解決方案，著重資源保存、最佳化、循環利用與再利用。 公司慶祝百年之際（見第 32 頁「慶祝一世紀的環境管理」專欄），正推動多項計畫，展示工業在發揮水回收與再利用潛能，以及實現此目標可採用的技術選項上，所能扮演的重要角色。其中一項最新計畫，是位於印度加濟阿巴德（Ghaziabad）的水回收與再利用設施。 ## 加濟阿巴德水回收與再利用計畫 位於北方邦加濟阿巴德、日處理量 40,000 立方公尺的三級處理逆滲透（Tertiary Treatment Reverse Osmosis，TTRO）廠，是混合年金模式（Hybrid Annuity Model）下的指標性倡議，體現廢水管理的循環經濟做法。該廠是 WABAG「製造水」（Manufactured Water）概念的實例；它處理市政使用後水，為產業提供可行、可靠且永續的水源。設施採用超濾（UF）與逆滲透（RO）技術，生產高品質非飲用水，並透過專用輸配水網絡供應 Sahibabad 工業區近 1,500 家工廠。 藉由減少對地下水的依賴，該廠每年節省逾 1,400 萬立方公尺淡水，直接回應該市嚴峻的缺水壓力。 該地區被國家綠色審裁法庭（National Green Tribunal，NGT）列為「黑色區」，長期面臨地下水位下降；因此，這項倡議對長期永續性至關重要。計畫具有 80% 承購保證，不僅確保財務可行，也為產業提供成本有效且可靠的替代水源。 TTRO 廠減少污水排入 Hindon 河；智慧水系統則可進行即時監測，最佳化用水並減少浪費。 ## 環境與財務皆可永續 加濟阿巴德 TTRO 合約保證為產業供水 15 年，展現對水資源短缺的韌性。設施依循零取水政策運作，確保遵守地方與中央法規。從經濟面來看，該廠的供水價格為每立方公尺 41 印度盧比（相當於每立方公尺 0.45 歐元），低於可比較設施，使其對產業具有財務吸引力。此外，強制使用 TTRO 處理水的要求，與國家和全球水安全目標一致，可提升永續用水意識，並為全球尋求採用永續、技術驅動水管理方案的城市建立先例。 WABAG 另一項示範計畫，是印度鋼鐵業規模最大的集中式零液體排放（centralised zero liquid discharge，CZLD）廠。此計畫為減少該部門仰賴淡水水源樹立重要先例。 ## Nagarnar 集中式零液體排放計畫 位於恰蒂斯加爾邦 Nagarnar、日處理量 4,320 立方公尺的 CZLD 廠，是印度鋼鐵業的標竿倡議，也是邁向永續水管理的重要一步。該廠由 WABAG 為 NMDC（前身為國家礦業開發公司）開發，Mecon India 擔任計畫顧問；透過先進處理程序，讓工業廢水得以全面回收，進一步落實 WABAG 的循環經濟方法。 設施處理 NMDC 年產 300 萬噸（3 MTPA）綜合鋼廠內 15 種不同來源的放流水，其中包括五股高總溶解固體流與氰化物、硫氰酸鹽、油脂及有機化合物等複雜污染物。該廠結合膜處理與多效蒸發（multiple effect evaporation，MEE），達成亮眼的 90% 水回收率。回收水於廠內冷卻系統再利用，大幅降低對淡水水源的依賴。 此外，MEE 系統會將濃縮排放流中的鹽類結晶化，藉此減少廢棄物並提升程序效率。這項封閉迴路解決方案消除對環境的排放，並確保符合日益嚴格的環境規範。 ## 環境與財務皆可永續 設施於 2023 年投運，試運轉期間面臨相當大挑戰，包括處理硫氰酸鹽濃度約 600 ppm、化學需氧量（COD）接近 800 ppm 的放流水；這些都是試運轉期間最難處理的水流之一。然而，該廠配置穩健的處理鏈，包含高速固體接觸澄清池、雙層濾料濾池、活性碳濾池、紫外線消毒、UF、RO 與 MEE，因而能持續穩定運作。 隨著水資源短缺加劇與法規壓力升高，CZLD 廠在零排放政策下，確保鋼廠能不中斷運作。它也為尋求提升用水效率、降低環境衝擊，並採取前瞻永續資源管理做法的類似工業生態系統，提供可擴充的典範。 作者 Josef Lahnsteiner 博士為 WABAG 集團技術與研發總監；BK Kumaran 為 VA Tech WABAG 技術支援與試運轉團隊主管。 ## 慶祝一世紀的環境管理 「一百年來，WABAG（現為 VA Tech WABAG）一直是全球水永續領域的重要力量，提供保障資源、保護生態系並促進經濟成長的解決方案。這段歷程由深厚的環境管理承諾所形塑，確保水資源仍可取得、再利用，並受到負責任的管理。 從建設每天觸及數百萬人生活的供水與廢水基礎設施，到協助產業與城市蓬勃發展，WABAG 在應對水與衛生挑戰上一直扮演關鍵角色。這份傳承已對全球社區留下長久影響，確保人們取得潔淨水，並強化水安全。 在 WABAG，我們相信水太過珍貴，不應只使用一次。此一理念推動我們倡導「製造水」概念──將海水與廢水（使用後水）轉化為可行且永續的資源。 我們的工廠採取資源回收模式，確保使用後水不再是負債，而是有價值的資產。透過整合先進海水淡化、使用後水處理與水再利用技術，我們降低對淡水的依賴、保存地下水，並減少環境衝擊。 展望未來，我們對開創創新和永續水解決方案的承諾仍然堅定。公司已在 25 個國家建置超過 6,500 座廠，持續突破「製造水」、循環經濟導向的資源回收、節能技術與具氣候韌性的基礎設施等領域的界限。我們的使命很清楚：創造一個水安全的世界，讓每一滴水都受到珍視、處理並以負責任的方式再利用。 對 WABAG 而言，過去一個世紀關乎保存資源、保護環境與驅動經濟；下一個世紀則關乎擴大這份影響，持續創新、領導，並為後代打造永續的未來。」 Rajiv Mittal，VA Tech WABAG 董事長暨董事總經理

The Source官方網頁原文（查詢日期：2026-09-10）

With the company behind them celebrating its 100th anniversary, two Indian projects highlight the opportunities around industrial applications of water reclamation and reuse. J Lahnsteiner and BK Kumaran outline the projects. Established in August 1924, WABAG (now VA Tech WABAG) has a rich history that spans more than a century, offering a range of sustainable water technology solutions focused on conservation, optimisation, recycling and the reuse of resources. As it celebrates its centenary (see panel, ‘Celebrating a century of environmental stewardship’, on page 32), the company is delivering projects that highlight the important role of industry in exploiting the potential of water reclamation and reuse, and the technology options for achieving this. One of the latest projects is a water reclamation and reuse facility in Ghaziabad, India. Ghaziabad water reclamation and reuse project The 40,000 m3/d Tertiary Treatment Reverse Osmosis (TTRO) plant in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, is a landmark initiative under the Hybrid Annuity Model, embodying a circular economy approach to wastewater management. A testament to WABAG’s ‘Manufactured Water’ concept, the plant provides industries with a viable, reliable, and sustainable water source by treating municipal used water. Using ultrafiltration (UF) and reverse osmosis (RO), the facility produces high-quality non-potable water, supplying nearly 1500 industries in the Sahibabad Industrial Estate through a dedicated transmission and distribution network. By reducing dependence on groundwater, the plant conserves more than 14 million m3 of freshwater annually, directly addressing the city’s severe water stress. Classified as a ‘dark zone’ by the National Green Tribunal (NGT), the region has faced declining groundwater levels, making this initiative crucial for long-term sustainability. With an 80% offtake guarantee, the project also ensures financial viability while offering industries a cost-effective and reliable alternative. The TTRO plant reduces discharge of sewage into the Hindon river and smart water systems enable real time monitoring, optimising consumption and minimising wastage. Environmentally and financially sustainable The Ghaziabad TTRO contract guarantees a 15-year water supply for industries, demonstrating resilience against water scarcity. Operating under a zero-extraction policy, it ensures compliance with local and federal regulations. And from an economic point of view, the plant offers water at a cheaper price (INR 41/m3 which is equivalent to €0.45/m3) than comparable facilities, making it financially attractive for industries. In addition, the mandated use of TTRO-treated water aligns with national and global water security goals, promoting awareness of sustainable water usage and setting a precedent for cities worldwide looking to embrace sustainable, technology-driven water management solutions. Another of WABAG’s showcase projects is the largest centralised zero liquid discharge (CZLD) plant in the Indian steel industry. This project sets a significant precedent in reducing the sector’s reliance on freshwater sources. Nagarnar Centralised Zero Liquid Discharge Project The 4320 m3/day CZLD plant at Nagarnar, Chhattisgarh, is a benchmark initiative in India’s steel industry and a major step towards sustainable water management. Developed by WABAG for NMDC (formerly the National Mineral Development Corporation), with Mecon India as the project consultant, the plant enables comprehensive recycling of industrial wastewater through an advanced treatment process, reinforcing WABAG’s circular economy approach. The facility treats effluent from 15 different sources within NMDC’s 3 MTPA (million tonnes per annum) integrated steel plant, including five high total dissolved solids streams and complex pollutants such as cyanides, thiocyanates, oil and grease, and organic compounds. Using a combination of membrane-based treatment and multiple effect evaporation (MEE), the plant achieves an impressive 90% water recovery rate. The recovered water is reused within the plant’s cooling system, significantly reducing reliance on freshwater sources. Additionally, the MEE system crystallises salts from reject streams, minimising waste and enhancing process efficiency. This closed-loop solution eliminates discharge to the environment and ensures compliance with increasingly stringent environmental norms. Environmentally and financially sustainable Commissioned in 2023, the facility faced considerable challenges, including the treatment of effluent streams with thiocyanate concentrations of around 600 ppm and chemical oxygen demand (COD) levels close to 800 ppm – among the most difficult to manage during commissioning. However, with a robust treatment train comprising a high-rate solid contact clarifier, a dual media filter, an activated carbon filter, UV disinfection, UF, RO, and MEE, the plant delivers consistent performance. With rising water scarcity and growing regulatory pressure, the CZLD plant ensures uninterrupted steel plant operations under a zero-discharge policy. It serves as a scalable model for similar industrial ecosystems looking to enhance water efficiency, minimise environmental impact, and embrace a future-ready, sustainable approach to resource management.
