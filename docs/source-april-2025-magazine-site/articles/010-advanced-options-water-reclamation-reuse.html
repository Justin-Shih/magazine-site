中文完整摘要說明

VA Tech WABAG以印度Ghaziabad等工業回收再利用專案，說明三級處理、逆滲透、蒸發與結晶等技術如何讓工業用水朝高回收、減少淡水依賴與減排放方向發展。

中文全文翻譯

本文介紹工業水回收與再利用的工程案例，重點是依原水與用途條件組合三級處理、逆滲透、蒸發濃縮與鹽類結晶。文章以高回收率與廠內冷卻水再用為例，凸顯工業部門可降低對淡水的依賴；但其成效與成本、能源、濃縮液處置、操作能力與用水需求密切相關，不能離開案例條件直接推論。

Nagarnar Centralised Zero Liquid Discharge Project The 4320 m3/day CZLD plant at Nagarnar, Chhattisgarh, is a benchmark initiative in India’s steel industry and a major step towards sustainable water management. Developed by WABAG for NMDC (formerly the National Mineral Development Corporation), with Mecon India as the project consultant, the plant enables comprehensive recycling of industrial wastewater through an advanced treatment process, reinforcing WABAG’s circular economy approach. The facility treats effluent from 15 different sources within NMDC’s 3 MTPA (million tonnes per annum) integrated steel plant, including five high total dissolved solids streams and complex pollutants such as cyanides, thiocyanates, oil and grease, and organic compounds.
